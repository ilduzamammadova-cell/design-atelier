# Guna mola

**Essence:** Colour revealed by cutting down through stacked layers of cloth, with every shape echoed by parallel channels until the panel is entirely alive.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Depth by subtraction | Several layers of coloured cloth are stacked; the maker cuts through upper layers and turns the edges under to expose colours beneath (reverse appliqué). | Layered UI built from 3 to 4 flat colour planes; elements "cut through" upper planes to reveal lower ones via masks. Depth comes from cut-outs, never drop shadows. |
| G2 · Echo contours | Main motifs are outlined by repeated parallel channels that follow the shape outward. | Offset-path outlines (2 to 4 parallel strokes at 4px spacing) around a logo, focus state or key illustration; the echo count signals importance. |
| G3 · No dead ground | Background areas are filled with slits, channels and small inserts, so the whole panel vibrates. | Dense patterned backgrounds allowed only inside a bounded panel (a card, a header); content areas outside stay plain for reading. |
| G4 · Paired panels | A blouse carries a front and a back mola, usually related but not identical. | Paired views (before/after, front/back, light/dark) designed as variations on one composition, not mirrored duplicates. |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** red #C1272D, orange #F28C28, black #111111, yellow #F4D03F, green #1E8449, blue #2471A3, magenta #C2185B.
- **Type:** none intrinsic; letters sometimes appear in contemporary molas as figures; for UI a rounded geometric sans.
- **Form:** labyrinthine channels, animals, plants, everyday objects, geometric mazes.
- **Composition:** rectangular panels filled edge to edge; strong central figures surrounded by echo lines.

## Attitude

Molas are worn daily by Guna women as part of their blouses and are a central sign of Guna identity, defended in the 1925 Guna revolution against forced assimilation. Making and selling molas is also a key source of income for many Guna women and families. The panel shows how layering, outline and colour can build complexity from simple cuts.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `henri-matisse`, colour by cutting, flat bright shapes.
- **Productive tension with:** `massimo-vignelli`, strict typographic order around one fully layered panel.
- **Clashes with:** `japanese-wabi-sabi-and-ma`, total-coverage chroma versus quiet void.

## Don't copy

Registered mola designs and individual molas in collections; the 2019 Nike "Puerto Rico" Air Force 1 design that drew Guna objections.

## Respect

The mola is protected as a collective right of the Guna under Panama's Law 20 of 2000; the right was registered in 2002, and non-Guna manufacture requires a licence and disclosure. In 2019 Nike withdrew an Air Force 1 shoe after Guna leaders objected to its unlicensed mola design; their leader said they did not oppose commercialisation, only use without consultation. Use the structural genes freely; for anything resembling a mola, license from Guna authorities and credit "Guna, Panama". Caricature is generic "tribal" prints and confusing Guna molas with other Central American textiles.
