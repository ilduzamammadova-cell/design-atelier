# Ndebele wall painting

**Essence:** Heavy black contours holding flat, brilliant colour on white walls, composed symmetrically around the doorway as a declaration of identity.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Black contour as structure | Thick black lines define every shape; colour sits inside like enamel in cloisons. | Components with 3–4px black borders, flat fills and no shadows; the outline network doubles as the grid. |
| G2 · Symmetry around the doorway | Homestead walls and gateways are painted symmetrically, with the entrance as the centre of the composition. | Landing and checkout screens centred on the primary action as "doorway", with mirrored ornamental bands framing it. |
| G3 · New objects absorbed into geometry | Painters drew modern things (buildings, stairs, light fittings, letters) into the geometric vocabulary. | Abstract your own product's objects (devices, documents, routes) into the pattern language, so ornament speaks of the present. |
| G4 · White ground, saturated fills | Lime-white walls carry reds, blues, yellows, greens and pinks at full strength. | White canvas with 4–5 saturated fills and black; no tints, no gradients. |
| G5 · Scale crescendo | On her 1991 BMW Art Car, Esther Mahlangu made shapes grow continuously from the front to the rear of the car to add a sense of motion. | Patterns that grow in scale along the direction of scroll or progress (small at start, large at completion). |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** black #111111, white #F8F8F5, red #D62828, blue #1D4E89, yellow #F6C90E, green #2A9D58, lilac #9C7BB8; earlier work used earth pigments.
- **Type:** letters appear inside some designs as geometric forms; for UI a bold geometric sans.
- **Form:** stepped shapes, chevrons, triangles, rectangles; stylised architecture.
- **Composition:** symmetrical panels and friezes around openings; strong bands at the base and top of walls.

## Attitude

The painting is women's work, passed from mother to daughter, and asserted Ndebele identity in a period when apartheid policy confined people to ethnically defined reserves. Esther Mahlangu (born c. 1935) carried it onto canvas, objects and, in 1991, a BMW 525i, as the first woman and first non-Western artist in the BMW Art Car series. The tradition is public, architectural and confident.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `de-stijl`, `memphis` — black line plus primaries, colour as structure.
- **Productive tension with:** `scandinavian-modern` — restrained furniture-like UI with one fully painted "wall".
- **Clashes with:** `japanese-wabi-sabi-and-ma` — muted void and saturated declaration undo each other.

## Don't copy

Esther Mahlangu's works including the 1991 BMW Art Car and her later commissions; work of Francina Ndimande and other named painters; specific homesteads.

## Respect

Ndebele painting is a living, women-held tradition; individual artists earn their living from it. Commission or license Ndebele artists and name them; do not market products as "Ndebele" unless they are made with Ndebele artists. Beadwork for marriage and initiation carries ceremonial meaning and is not décor. Caricature is generic "African" bright triangles detached from any maker or place.
