# Ronan and Erwan Bouroullec

**Essence:** Soft modularity, small, gentle elements that people assemble into their own airy structures and micro-architectures.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Grow-your-own structure | *Algues* (Vitra, 2004) are injection-moulded plastic branch-like elements that clip together endlessly into screens, curtains or partitions; the user decides the scale and density. | **Product:** a single small element with a connector that tiles in many directions. **UI:** a block-based editor where one primitive (a "leaf" block) connects in multiple directions; users grow layouts organically, and density emerges from use rather than templates. |
| G2 · Textile tiles as architecture | *North Tiles* (Kvadrat, 2007) and *Clouds* (Kvadrat, 2009) are folded fabric tiles joined by rubber bands into soft walls that absorb sound and divide space. | **Product:** soft panels joined by visible, simple connectors. **UI:** panel tiles joined by small visible "band" connectors (4px rounded joints in a contrasting tone), letting users snap and rearrange; panels soft-cornered with a fabric-like matte fill. |
| G3 · Micro-architecture | *Lit Clos* (2000) is a raised enclosed bed, a room within a room; the *Alcove* sofa (Vitra, 2006) uses high soft walls to create privacy in open offices. | **Product:** furniture that creates a pocket of privacy inside a larger space. **UI:** focus pockets, a panel that raises soft "walls" (dimmed surroundings, enclosing frame) around a task within a busy workspace; exit restores the open view. |
| G4 · Shared table as system | *Joyn* (Vitra, 2002) is a long shared table system that reorganised office work around one surface with plug-in accessories. | **Product:** one large shared base with modular add-ons. **UI:** a shared canvas or document where tools mount as attachments along an edge (a rail with add-on panels), rather than separate apps. |
| G5 · Consumer tech, softened | The *Serif* TV (Samsung, 2015) gives a television a profile shaped like a serif letter "I" and a stance on legs, so it reads as furniture. | **Product:** a household tech object with a furniture silhouette and a "stance". **UI:** device-companion UIs that speak in domestic, furniture-like terms, warm neutrals, soft panels, an "ambient" mode showing art or information when idle. |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** soft desaturated tones, moss (#7F8C5A), dusty blue (#7C93A6), sand (#D8C9AE), terracotta (#C0694B), charcoal (#3A3A3A), white plastic.
- **Type:** not typographic; their drawings are loose felt-tip. For their register use Figtree or DM Sans for labels; a hand font only for sketches.
- **Form:** organic branches, folded textile tiles, soft high-walled sofas, thin steel and wood, rounded rectangles.
- **Composition:** repetition of small elements into large fields; airy, permeable partitions.

## Attitude

The brothers believe that objects should leave space for use and appropriation, and that a lightness of presence makes environments more humane. Drawing is central to their practice; their public work (Champs-Élysées roundabout fountains, 2019) extends the same softness into the city.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `hella-jongerius`, `scandinavian-modern`, `jasper-morrison`.
- **Productive tension with:** `joe-colombo`, Colombo's self-contained living machines softened into grow-your-own textile and plastic fields.
- **Clashes with:** `brutalism`, mass and rawness smother their permeable softness.

## Don't copy

The *Algues* element shape, *Clouds* and *North Tiles* fold geometries, *Vegetal* chair, *Serif* TV profile, *Lit Clos*. Take grow-your-own modularity and visible soft connectors.
