# Jonathan Ive

**Essence:** Seamless objects where material, process and interface merge — the sense that the product was not assembled but simply exists.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Unibody: one block, no seams | From 2008 Apple's MacBook enclosures were milled from a single block of aluminium, removing parts and seams; the process became the brand's look of precision. | **Product:** one-piece enclosure, minimal parting lines, process as aesthetic. **UI:** a single continuous surface per screen — no nested bordered boxes; groups separated by spacing and subtle tonal shifts (2–3% lightness) instead of outlines; one material token for all chrome. |
| G2 · Continuous curvature | Apple hardware and icons moved from simple circular-arc corners to continuous-curvature ("squircle"-like) transitions, so highlights flow without a visible break. | **Product:** corners with curvature continuity, no visible tangent break. **UI:** use superellipse/continuous corners for containers and icons (e.g. CSS `corner-shape: squircle` where supported, or SVG masks); keep a single corner family across icon, card and device frame. |
| G3 · Translucency to reveal the inside | The Bondi Blue iMac G3 (1998) used translucent coloured plastic so people could glimpse the internals — making the computer approachable and less intimidating. | **Product:** a translucent shell that shows structure and invites touch. **UI:** layered translucent materials (background blur with a tint of the content behind) so the user always sees where they are in the stack; a "peek" state that reveals underlying structure. |
| G4 · One physical gesture for an infinite list | The iPod (2001) and its later click wheel mapped endless scrolling through thousands of songs onto a circular thumb gesture with acceleration. | **Product:** one continuous physical control for the dominant navigation task. **UI:** a single direct-manipulation control for the main navigation — scrubbable timeline, rotary crown, momentum scroll with acceleration curves — tuned until it feels physical (inertia decay ~0.998 per ms). |
| G5 · Obsession with the invisible | Ive's team cared about parts users rarely see (internal layouts, packaging, the feel of a hinge), treating care itself as a quality people sense. | **Product:** finish the inside to the same standard as the outside; design the unboxing. **UI:** polish the invisible states — focus rings, loading skeletons, error copy, reduced-motion alternatives, the first 300 ms of launch — to the same standard as hero screens. |
| G6 · Deference of chrome to content | The 2013 iOS 7 redesign, overseen by Ive's group, removed skeuomorphic textures in favour of flat layers, translucency and type, so content led. | **Product:** the device's frame recedes behind the screen. **UI:** controls as plain text or thin glyphs, content full-bleed; chrome uses translucency and weight, not boxes and gradients. |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** aluminium silver (#D6D7D9), space grey (#4B4C50), white polycarbonate (#F5F5F7), Bondi Blue translucent (#0095B6), soft black glass (#0B0B0C).
- **Type:** Apple's Myriad then San Francisco; LoveFrom later commissioned LoveFrom Serif. Free equivalents: Albert Sans, Figtree; for a calm serif, Newsreader.
- **Form:** milled aluminium, continuous-curvature corners, glass, translucency, chamfered edges (iPhone 5 era), tiny tolerances.
- **Composition:** a single dominant surface; symmetry; everything aligned to one front plane.

## Attitude

Ive's team believed products should express respect for the people who use them, and that care shows up as simplicity — not the absence of clutter but the clarity of purpose. After leaving Apple in 2019 he founded LoveFrom with Marc Newson; from 2025 LoveFrom also leads design work with OpenAI following OpenAI's acquisition of the hardware company io.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `dieter-rams`, `bang-and-olufsen`, `naoto-fukasawa`.
- **Productive tension with:** `hella-jongerius` — machined perfection with her imperfect colour and textile craft yields precise devices with humane, varied surfaces.
- **Clashes with:** `memphis` — pattern-on-pattern clashes with seamless monolithic surfaces.

## Don't copy

The iMac G3, iPod click wheel, iPhone and Apple Watch silhouettes, Apple icon shapes and the Apple logo; Apple trade dress is aggressively protected. Take unibody thinking, continuous curvature and deference.
