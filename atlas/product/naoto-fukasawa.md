# Naoto Fukasawa

**Essence:** Design that dissolves into behaviour — objects shaped to fit what people already do without thinking.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Without Thought | His "Without Thought" workshops studied unconscious actions (where people rest a cup, how they hook a bag) and designed for them; the idea is to find the action people already perform and give it a home. | **Product:** observe unconscious habits and shape the object to receive them. **UI:** put actions where attention already is — swipe-to-archive on the row, inline edit on double-click, keyboard shortcuts that mirror OS habits; validate with users who are not told what to look for. |
| G2 · The familiar gesture borrowed | The wall-mounted CD player for Muji (1999, MoMA collection) plays when you pull a cord, like a kitchen ventilation fan; the motion of the disc echoes a fan's spin. | **Product:** map a new function onto an old, familiar gesture. **UI:** new features triggered by an existing mental model — pull-to-refresh, a dial for time, a drawer for hidden tools — and the visual response mirrors the metaphor (spin, slide, open). |
| G3 · The outline (rinkaku) | Fukasawa speaks of finding the contour an object "should" have — the shape that sits between existing archetypes and feels just right, neither novel nor generic. | **Product:** iterate the silhouette until it feels inevitable; avoid both cliché and novelty. **UI:** components refined at the level of proportion: button height-to-padding ratios, corner radius relative to height (radius = height × 0.25), icon optical sizes; small decisions that make the familiar feel right. |
| G4 · Just right (±0) | He founded the ±0 (Plus Minus Zero) brand in 2003 for household goods that feel neither too much nor too little; the INFOBAR phone for KDDI (2003) used colour tiles on a slim bar. | **Product:** calibrate everything to a neutral midpoint, then add one quiet character note. **UI:** neutral system with one gentle personality note (e.g. tile-like key colours in a keypad or a soft accent for the active tab), never more than one. |
| G5 · Trace of use | Designs such as a tray with a stain-like ring for a cup, or an umbrella stand reduced to a groove in the floor, start from the mark use leaves behind. | **Product:** design from the trace an activity leaves. **UI:** show traces of use — recently touched items softly highlighted, worn paths (most-used commands) surfacing first; history that feels like a footprint, not a log. |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** soft whites (#F5F3EE), warm greys (#BDB8AE), light woods (#D8C3A2), muted single accents (INFOBAR-style tile colours such as #E2483C, #2E7AB8 used sparingly).
- **Type:** quiet Japanese and Latin sans. Free equivalents: Noto Sans JP, Zen Kaku Gothic New, Figtree.
- **Form:** softened prisms, gentle radii, rounded-rectangle plans, minimal parting lines.
- **Composition:** objects sit low and quietly; proportions near squares and golden ratios; nothing protrudes.

## Attitude

Fukasawa believes good design is felt rather than noticed; it fits the environment and the body so well that people use it without thinking. With Jasper Morrison he defined "Super Normal" (exhibition, Axis Gallery, Tokyo, 2006) — the extraordinary quality of ordinary objects.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `jasper-morrison`, `kenya-hara`, `henry-dreyfuss`.
- **Productive tension with:** `achille-castiglioni` — both observe behaviour; Castiglioni adds wit, Fukasawa adds silence.
- **Clashes with:** `philippe-starck` — attention-seeking character objects against design that disappears.

## Don't copy

The Muji wall-mounted CD player, INFOBAR's tiled keypad, ±0 humidifier forms, the Hiroshima chair (Maruni). Take Without Thought and trace-of-use.
