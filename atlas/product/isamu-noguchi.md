# Isamu Noguchi

**Essence:** Sculpture that agrees to be useful, light, tables and landscapes shaped as quiet presences rather than appliances.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Light as a glowing volume | Akari light sculptures (from 1951, made with Gifu lantern artisans using washi paper over bamboo ribbing) turn a bulb into a soft volume of light. They collapse flat for shipping. | **Product:** diffuse the source through a translucent skin; collapse flat. **UI:** light from within, shown with flat value steps, the active or focused surface turns one step lighter and warmer (paper lit from behind), never a glow, gradient or blur; a faint ribbing of 1px hairlines at a fixed interval, used once, as the lantern's bamboo. Avoid backdrop blur and translucent panels: that is glassmorphism, not paper. |
| G2 · Two shapes in balance | The glass-topped coffee table for Herman Miller (1947) rests on two identical interlocking wooden pieces; balance and weight are visibly negotiated. | **Product:** structure from two counterweighted parts. **UI:** asymmetric two-column layouts balanced by visual weight (a heavy image block against a light text column of equal optical mass), not by equal widths. |
| G3 · Landscape as interface | Gardens (UNESCO, Paris, 1956 to 58; California Scenario, 1980 to 82) and playgrounds (Playscapes, Atlanta, 1975 to 76; Moerenuma Park, Sapporo, opened 2005) shape ground itself into something people move through and use. | **Product:** treat the ground plane as a designed surface. **UI:** spatial, explorable canvases where content is placed on a "terrain" (zoomable map, mood board) and the user navigates by moving, not paging; paths implied by spacing rather than drawn arrows. |
| G4 · The useful single industrial object | His Radio Nurse baby monitor for Zenith (1937) wraps electronics in a mask-like Bakelite form, technology given a face and a presence. | **Product:** a sculpted, characterful housing for one domestic technology. **UI:** a device or assistant represented by one sculptural, softly animated object (an orb, a stone) with presence states (idle breathe 4 s cycle, listening, speaking) rather than a robotic avatar. |
| G5 · Natural material, minimal intervention | Stone carvings and wood pieces often keep raw faces next to polished ones. | **Product:** show the material's untouched surface beside the finished one. **UI:** pair a crisp UI layer with one raw, photographed material texture (paper, stone) used as a background plate at low contrast. |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** washi white (#F3EEE3), bamboo (#C6A66B), basalt (#3A3A38), granite grey (#8A8580), walnut (#5C3A21), warm light (#FFE7B8).
- **Type:** not a type designer; Akari packaging uses simple sans. For his register: Shippori Mincho or Noto Serif JP for quiet titles, Zen Kaku Gothic New for labels.
- **Form:** biomorphic and stone-like volumes, soft ribs, interlocking organic pieces, carved landforms.
- **Composition:** objects placed in space like stones in a garden, few, deliberate, with large intervals.

## Attitude

Noguchi wanted sculpture to leave the pedestal and enter daily life, public space and the ground itself. He refused borders between art, design and landscape, and between Japanese and American traditions.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `kenya-hara`, `naoto-fukasawa`, `charles-and-ray-eames`.
- **Productive tension with:** `dieter-rams`, Rams' engineered calm with Noguchi's organic glow gives precise products with a soft, luminous presence.
- **Clashes with:** `memphis`, Memphis' loud laminates overwhelm Noguchi's quiet material presence.

## Don't copy

The Noguchi coffee table base, specific Akari shapes (e.g. 1A, 10A, the tall column models) and their ribbing patterns, the Radio Nurse mask, the Freeform sofa outline. Take glow-from-within and balanced asymmetry.
