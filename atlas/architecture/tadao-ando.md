# Tadao Ando

**Essence:** Silky concrete boxes in which a single cut of light, a courtyard of sky or a sheet of water becomes the only event.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Light slot | In the Church of the Light (Ibaraki, 1989) a cross is cut clean through the concrete wall; it is the only bright thing in a dim room. | One narrow accent of light on a dark, concrete-toned surface: background ≈ #2B2A28, a single 2 to 4 px line or slit in warm near-white (≈ #F4EFE3) marks the one primary action or focus. No other highlight exists on that screen. |
| G2 · The approach path | Visitors walk around walls, turn and descend before they arrive (at the Water Temple on Awaji, 1991, the stair goes down through a lotus pond). The route resets the senses. | A deliberate short approach before the main content: a quiet threshold screen, or a scroll sequence of 2 to 3 turns (blocks alternating left and right) that delays the reveal. Always user-paced; never auto-advance. |
| G3 · The tie-hole grid | In-situ concrete cast in panels of tatami proportion (about 90 × 180 cm), each with six tie holes; the grid of dots and seams is the only ornament. | A barely visible construction grid: a dot matrix aligned to the layout module (3 to 4 px dots at about 3% contrast, panels in a 1:2 rhythm), panel seams as 1 px lines. All content snaps to that same grid. |
| G4 · Nature let in, framed | In the Row House in Sumiyoshi (1976) you cross an open courtyard to go from room to room, even in rain. Sky, wind and water are the decoration. | Leave one region of the interface open to the live outside: real weather, time-of-day light, a live feed. Everything around it stays austere; the changing element is the only ornament. |
| G5 · Pure geometry | Squares, circles and long straight walls that slice space at precise angles. | Shapes limited to squares, 1:2 rectangles and at most one circle per view; square corners; a single angled wall (one rule at an exact angle) may cut the composition. |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** smooth concrete greys (≈ #A9A69F light, #6F6C67 mid, #2B2A28 shadow), warm daylight (≈ #F4EFE3), dark water (≈ #1F2A2E), dark-stained timber (≈ #4E3E31).
- **Type:** none; type recedes → free: "Hanken Grotesk" light or "Noto Sans JP" light, small and widely tracked.
- **Form:** fair-faced concrete with tie holes, walls as independent planes, slits, glass, still water.
- **Composition:** walls cut space into sequences; central courtyards; strict axial or rotated geometries.

## Attitude

Ando, a former boxer, taught himself architecture by travelling and studying buildings, Le Corbusier's above all. He uses the austere box to confront people bodily with light, wind and water, which everyday city life numbs. His minimalism removes distraction so that elemental experience can take the stage. Pritzker Prize 1995.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `louis-kahn`, `kenya-hara`, silence, emptiness, light as material.
- **Productive tension with:** `luis-barragan`, grey concrete calm with one Barragán color wall; the concrete makes the color sing.
- **Clashes with:** `memphis`, playful clutter and many colors cancel the single event that gives Ando's space its force.

## Don't copy

The cross-shaped light slot of the Church of the Light (a sacred and signature image); the Row House façade; the plan of the Chichu Art Museum.
