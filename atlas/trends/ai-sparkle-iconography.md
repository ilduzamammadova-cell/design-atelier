# AI Sparkle Iconography

**Essence:** The four-pointed star cluster (✨), often filled with a violet-to-blue gradient, stamped on every button, menu item and badge that touches AI.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Disclose machine involvement | Users should know when content is generated or an action is automated. | Label the action in words ("Draft reply", "Summarise thread") and mark generated content with a persistent text provenance line ("Generated — review before sending"), not only an icon. |
| G2 · Set expectations | A distinct marker separates probabilistic features from deterministic ones. | Verb-specific icon + short label (pen for draft, list for summary); show what will happen before it runs — preview, diff, one-step undo. |
| G3 · Invite exploration | "Magic" framing lowers the barrier to trying something new. | Invite with examples, not glitter: three concrete suggested prompts drawn from the user's current context. |

## Why it reads as slop now

Sparkles meant "clean, new, special" long before AI — cleaning-product branding used them first. Google's UX researchers trace the AI use to a 2016 Explore icon in Docs, Sheets and Slides; Google standardised its sparkle in 2023 and had nearly 100 sparkle icons across products by 2024. Microsoft, Adobe, Zoom, Notion and countless startups followed, often with the same gradient. Google's own researchers note that ubiquity blurs meaning: people can't tell what a sparkle button will do or what kind of AI is involved. The cheap version: a gradient ✨ before "Ask AI", "Generate", "Enhance" and "New", plus a sparkle in the hero badge, with no description of the actual action.

## Subvert

- **Name the verb.** Replace the generic sparkle with the action's own icon and label; AI is the implementation, the outcome is the feature.
- **Design a provenance mark.** If the product needs an AI marker, draw a specific, modest, monochrome glyph inside your icon system (a corner tick, a bracket) and define exactly where it may appear.
- **Show the machine honestly.** A visible draft state — dashed outline, monospace metadata, model name, timestamp — communicates generated content more truthfully than magic.

## Visual facts

- **Color:** gradient fills (violet #8B5CF6 → blue #3B82F6 → pink) or multi-color; sometimes an animated shimmer.
- **Type:** n/a — paired with labels like "Ask AI", "Magic", "Enhance".
- **Form:** one large + one or two small four-pointed stars with concave sides.
- **Composition:** leading icon in buttons, chips and inputs; badge inside hero pills.

## Attitude

AI as magic — wonder rather than mechanism. It sells delight and hides what the system is actually doing.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `purple-blue-gradient-saas`, `dark-mode-neon-glow`, `centered-hero-three-cards-template` (the "✨ New" badge).
- **Productive tension with:** `susan-kare`, `otl-aicher` — pictograms designed to mean one precise thing.
- **Clashes with:** `editorial-serif-revival` — confetti glyphs against a literate, sober voice.

## Don't copy

Google Gemini's spark, Microsoft Copilot's mark and other AI product logos; the Unicode ✨ emoji used as a brand logo.
