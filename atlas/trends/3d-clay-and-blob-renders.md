# 3D Clay & Blob Renders

**Essence:** Soft, matte, toy-like 3D objects — inflated icons, pastel coins and padlocks, rounded characters, wobbly abstract blobs — studio-lit and floating on plain backgrounds.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Friendly tactility | Rounded volume and soft light make abstract services (money, security, data) feel safe and huggable. | Deliver tactility through interaction: buttons with a 2px press offset and an 80ms spring return; a haptic tick on mobile confirmations. |
| G2 · Object metaphor | A single object (lock, coin, rocket) stands for a feature at a glance. | Choose objects from the product's real world and draw them in one consistent 2D icon grid (24px, 1.5px stroke); at most one hero object per page. |
| G3 · Delight moments | A playful render rewards milestones and softens errors. | Reserve illustration for three moments only — first success, empty state, error — each with copy that says what to do next. |

## Why it reads as slop now

Affordable Blender and Cinema 4D workflows, then browser tools like Spline, made pastel 3D renders the Dribbble look of 2020–2022. Michal Malewicz named "claymorphism" in 2021 for inflated UI elements with double inner shadows; Microsoft showed 3D Fluent emoji the same year; fintech and crypto apps filled up with floating coins and padlocks. Now image generators produce "3D clay icon, pastel" on request, so the look costs nothing and is everywhere. The cheap version: a glossy pastel rocket or padlock tilted 15° above a card titled "Secure by default", soft contact shadow, no relation to the rest of the product's visual system.

## Subvert

- **Real objects, real photos.** Photograph physical objects relevant to the product as a consistent series — honest materiality instead of synthetic clay.
- **Handmade 3D.** Actual clay, paper or felt models shot simply (stop-motion warmth) keep the tactile need and show human labor.
- **Flat with one depth cue.** Keep icons 2D and add a single system-wide extrusion (2px offset in a darker brand tone) — dimensionality as a detail, not a render.

## Visual facts

- **Color:** pastels — lavender #C4B5FD, mint #A7F3D0, peach #FECACA, butter #FDE68A — plus one saturated accent.
- **Type:** rounded geometric sans (Nunito, Poppins, DM Sans).
- **Form:** 20–40px radius inflated shapes, inner highlight + inner shadow, ambient occlusion, matte plastic or clay material.
- **Composition:** one object floating centered or breaking out of a card edge.

## Attitude

Technology should feel like a toy — harmless, approachable, fun. A kind instinct, but it turns serious products (money, health, security) into candy.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `spline-3d-hero`, `neumorphism`, `emoji-as-icons`.
- **Productive tension with:** `dieter-rams` — product honesty forces the object metaphor to look like a real, useful thing.
- **Clashes with:** `swiss-international` — rendered toys inside a strict typographic grid look pasted in.

## Don't copy

Microsoft Fluent 3D emoji used as brand icons; commercial 3D icon packs as an identity; Malewicz's claymorphism showcase shots.
