# webOS (Palm)

**Essence:** A phone as a deck of live cards, fanned, shuffled and flicked away, with notifications that wait politely at the bottom edge.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Cards as live tasks | Each running app shrank into a card you could see, reorder, and flick upward to close; later versions grouped related cards into stacks. | Open items appear as live-content thumbnails in a horizontal deck; flick up to dismiss, drag to reorder, drop one card onto another to stack. The task switcher *is* the home. |
| G2 · Notifications that wait | Alerts slid in as a thin strip at the bottom and collapsed into small icons, never blocking work. | Non-modal notifications enter as a ≤40 px bottom strip, then collapse into a row of icons; expand on tap. Nothing covers the centre of the screen unless the user asks. |
| G3 · A gesture zone | A dedicated gesture area below the screen accepted swipes (back, open the card view), with a glowing light bar as feedback. | Reserve one edge band for system gestures with its own visual feedback (a light bar that brightens along the swipe path); keep app content gestures out of it. |
| G4 · Merge by person, not source | "Synergy" combined contacts, calendars and messages from several accounts into one view. | Aggregate the same entity from many sources into one record with small source badges; never make users browse per-account lists. |
| G5 · Just type | Typing from the home view began a universal search across apps, contacts, web and actions. | Any keystroke on the home view opens search pre-filled; results grouped by type with actions ("Call", "New email") as first-class results. |
| G6 · Smoked glass and rounded masks | Translucent dark panels, white light-weight type and rounded screen corners echoed the pebble-shaped hardware. | Menus as smoked glass (`#000` at 70% with slight blur); light-weight humanist type in white; masked rounded corners on every surface, including full-screen views. |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** charcoal and black surfaces, translucent greys, white type; soft light-glow accents rather than saturated hues.
- **Type:** Prelude, a custom humanist sans made for webOS. Free: **Source Sans 3** or **Lato** (light and regular weights).
- **Form:** rounded cards, pebble curves, soft glows, dark translucency, minimal borders.
- **Composition:** full-screen app cards; a launcher sheet of icons; a bottom "dashboard" of collapsed notifications.

## Attitude

Matías Duarte led webOS's human interface (announced at CES in January 2009, launched on the Palm Pre that June). The belief: people live in many simultaneous activities, and a physical metaphor, cards on a table, makes that manageable without a manual. HP bought Palm in 2010 and halted devices in 2011, but the card idea spread through mobile multitasking, and Duarte carried it to Google (`material-design`).

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `material-design`, `palm-os`, `ambient-and-e-ink-ui`, cards, calm notifications, quiet presence.
- **Productive tension with:** `classic-macintosh`, HyperCard's stack-of-cards authoring meeting webOS's live card deck suggests user-composable, stackable workspaces.
- **Clashes with:** `windows-95`, a taskbar of labelled buttons and a spatial card deck are two incompatible models of "what is open".

## Don't copy

- webOS and Palm logos, Prelude's letterforms, the Palm Pre's hardware form, webOS system icons and wallpapers.
