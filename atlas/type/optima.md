# Optima

**Essence:** A sans serif with the bones of a Roman inscription. Strokes swell gently towards their ends instead of carrying serifs, so the letters feel carved, calm and humane.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Flare instead of serif | Stroke endings widen slightly (a glyphic flare), keeping the memory of a chisel without serifs. | The heading face for "humane premium" products: 20 to 48px, weight 500, tracking +1%. At UI-label sizes (13 to 15px) it behaves as a sans with a softer edge than any grotesk. |
| G2 · Waisted strokes | Stems taper gently towards their middles. It looks elegant in print and fragile on coarse screens. | Minimum 13px for regular, 18px for light weights. On 1× Windows prefer medium. Test the narrowing in dark mode, where thin parts shrink further. |
| G3 · Roman proportions in a sans | Zapf based it on Renaissance tomb lettering in Florence and classical capital proportions: wide round letters, narrow *E, F, S*. | Lists of names (memorials, credits, donors, teams) as caps at +5 to 8% tracking, one name per line, on a shared vertical axis. Ordering, not decoration, carries the dignity. |
| G4 · From sloped roman to true italic | The 1958 Optima had only a sloped roman. Optima nova (2002) added the true italic Zapf had wanted from the start. | Specify a true italic for emphasis. If a family only has obliques, mark emphasis with weight or colour instead of slant. |
| G5 · Golden-section vertical metrics | Zapf said its x-height and extender lengths follow the golden section. | Derive display steps from one ratio (e.g. 1.618: 16 → 26 → 42 → 68px) while body steps stay small (×1.125). One proportion governs the whole hierarchy. |

## Visual facts

- **Classification:** humanist sans with flared (glyphic) terminals, a serif/sans hybrid.
- **Origin:** Hermann Zapf, inspired by Renaissance inscriptions in Florence seen in 1950; developed 1955 to 58; released by D. Stempel AG, Frankfurt, in 1958, then by Mergenthaler Linotype. Zapf wanted to call it "New Roman".
- **Main digital versions:** Optima (Linotype; ships with macOS). Optima nova (2002, Zapf with Akira Kobayashi: seven weights with true italics, condensed and titling). Optima Pro Cyrillic (2010). All commercial.
- **Anatomy:** moderate x-height, open apertures, no serifs but swelling stroke ends, gentle contrast, tapered stems, classical capital widths.
- **Free / open alternatives:** URW Classico (a free clone); Linux Biolinum and Libertinus Sans (OFL; humanist sans with slight flare). Marcellus (Astigmatic, OFL) keeps the inscriptional flare in a serif form for display.

## Attitude

Zapf wanted one letter that could do both text and display: classical in proportion, modern in the absence of serifs. Optima argues that the sans serif need not be industrial; it can be written and carved.

## Using it today

- **Signals:** the Vietnam Veterans Memorial names (1982) and the 9/11 Memorial names, the Expo 67 identity, John McCain's 2008 campaign. It is also a frequent choice for beauty and wellness packaging. Critics have called it mannered or "down-market luxury". Saturation is moderate, with a 1980s to 90s luxury aftertaste.
- **Robotic ↔ humane: 7/10.** Calligraphic modulation, open apertures and flared endings feel human. Its symmetrical restraint and polish keep it formal.
- **UI fitness:** labels ≥ 13px (regular or medium); body works for short texts at 16 to 18px; display good. Rendering: stroke tapering thins on low-DPI screens. Coverage: Optima nova is Latin, with a separate Cyrillic; check Greek.
- **Pairs with:** Palatino, by the same hand, as the text serif. EB Garamond for literary reading. Trajan or Cinzel for occasional inscriptional titles.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `hermann-zapf` and `palatino`: the same calligrapher's system; flare and pen in harmony.
- **Productive tension with:** `swiss-international`: a flared, humane sans placed on a strict grid instead of a grotesk gives rational structure with a softer voice.
- **Clashes with:** `neo-brutalism-web`: hard black borders and offset shadows crush its subtle stroke modulation.

## Don't copy

Optima and Optima nova outlines without licence; memorial wall layouts as decoration; cosmetics brand wordmarks set in Optima.
