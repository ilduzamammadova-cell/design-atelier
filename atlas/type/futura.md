# Futura (1927)

**Essence:** Compass-and-ruler letters for "a typeface of our time", pure circles, triangles and squares, then quietly corrected by eye so the geometry reads as grace rather than diagram.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Construction from primitives | Renner built the lowercase from circle, triangle and square: a near-circular o, sharp-apex A/M/N, single-storey a. The forms announce a rational, forward-looking worldview. | Reserve a strict geometric face for display (≥ 28px) and wordmarks; derive the icon set from the same primitives (circle, 45°/60° triangle, square) with matching stroke so type and icons share one geometry. |
| G2 · Optical correction under the geometry | The circles are not perfect and strokes vary subtly; Bauer also adapted cuts for different sizes. Geometry is the idea, the eye is the judge. | When building geometric UI shapes, overshoot circles 1 to 2% beyond the square they align with, and make horizontals ~5% thinner than verticals; test icons at 16 and 24px, not only at 512. |
| G3 · Tall ascenders, small x-height | Ascenders rise above the cap height and the x-height is low, giving an elegant, airy line that needs space. | If you use a Futura-like face for text, set body ≥ 18px with line-height 1.5 to 1.6 and max 60ch; for UI labels at 12 to 14px use a companion face with a larger x-height. |
| G4 · Pointed apexes as signature | The sharp A, M, N, V, W apexes give headlines a crisp silhouette, especially in caps. | Uppercase headlines in a sharp-apex geometric at +4 to 8% tracking for luxury/editorial tone; keep sentence case for anything longer than five words. |

## Visual facts

- **Classification:** geometric sans.
- **Designer · year · foundry:** Paul Renner, Bauer Type Foundry, Frankfurt, 1927 (project begun 1924; Heinrich Jost was Bauer's art director). Contemporary rival: Erbar (Ludwig & Mayer).
- **Digital versions & licences:** Futura PT (ParaType; Vladimir Yefimov & Isabella Chaeva, 1990s, revised 2007 and 2022, Latin + Cyrillic), versions from URW, Bitstream, Linotype/Monotype; Futura ships with macOS. All commercial. Spartan (Mergenthaler/ATF, 1939) was an American near-copy.
- **Proportions:** small x-height, tall ascenders, near-circular bowls, open geometric apertures, monoline strokes, single-storey a; obliques rather than true italics.
- **Free / open alternatives (keep the gene):** Jost (Owen Earl / indestructible type, OFL, Futura-inspired, original drawing, variable); League Spartan (The League of Moveable Type, OFL, revival of ATF Spartan, variable 200 to 900).

## Attitude

Renner wanted letters freed from historical handwriting and fit for a new, machine-age society. The underlying belief: form should come from clear construction, not inherited ornament, yet he trusted the eye over the compass.

## Using it today

- **Signals:** Volkswagen ads from the 1960s, the Apollo 11 lunar plaque, Kubrick and Wes Anderson title cards, luxury fashion, IKEA catalogues before 2009. Saturation: high in fashion/lifestyle; reads "mid-century chic" or "space-age".
- **Robotic ↔ humane:** 3/10, circles and triangles are ideal, not human; there is no pen logic, and the obliques are slanted geometry. Its elegance is cool, aristocratic rather than warm.
- **UI fitness:** display, wordmarks, short navigation in caps; weak for body and small labels (low x-height, similar a/o/e circles at small sizes). Minimum 14px for UI labels; body 18px+ only for short texts. Futura PT and Jost include Cyrillic, check extended Latin per version.
- **Pairs with:** Source Sans 3 or Fira Sans for UI text, large x-height fixes Futura's small-size weakness; Bodoni or Didot for fashion-editorial body and deck (old-world contrast against new-world geometry); Garamond for literary reading.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `bauhaus`, `herbert-bayer`, `jan-tschichold`, construction-first modernism.
- **Productive tension with:** `kenya-hara`, `japanese-wabi-sabi-and-ma`, rational geometry set sparingly in vast white space reads as calm luxury.
- **Clashes with:** `victorian-wood-type`, geometric purity against ornamental fat-face loudness reads as costume.

## Don't copy

Futura outlines (a German court ruling assigns the copyright to Renner and his heirs); the Apollo plaque layout; famous wordmarks set in Futura (VW-era ads, Supreme, Louis Vuitton).
