# Sources, graphic design c.1945 to 1975 (graphic-midcentury)

Strongest resources for studying the Swiss style, the New York School, corporate identity, the conceptual poster and the 1960s alternatives.

## Books

- **Philip B. Meggs & Alston W. Purvis, *Meggs' History of Graphic Design*, 5th ed. (2011), chs. 18 to 21**, the standard survey; chapters on the International Typographic Style, New York School, corporate identity and the conceptual image are the backbone of this section.
- **Jeremy Aynsley, *A Century of Graphic Design* (2001), "Mid-Century Modern" and "Pop, Subversion and Alternatives"**, compact designer-by-designer entries with well-chosen plates; good for comparing Europe, America and Japan side by side.
- **Josef Müller-Brockmann, *Grid Systems in Graphic Design* (1981)**, the working manual of modular grids; directly translatable to layout tokens.
- **Emil Ruder, *Typographie: A Manual of Design* (1967)**, typography as texture, rhythm and counterform; the deepest Swiss text on type.
- **Armin Hofmann, *Graphic Design Manual: Principles and Practice* (1965)**, elementary form exercises (point, line, plane, contrast); a teaching method still usable today.
- **Karl Gerstner, *Designing Programmes* (1964; English 1968)**, rule-based, parametric design decades before code; essential for design-system thinking.
- **Richard Hollis, *Swiss Graphic Design: The Origins and Growth of an International Style 1920 to 1965* (2006)**, the best critical history of the Swiss movement.
- **Paul Rand, *Thoughts on Design* (1947) and *A Designer's Art* (1985)**, the American modernist argument for form, function and play, in the designer's own words.
- **Jennifer Bass & Pat Kirkham, *Saul Bass: A Life in Film & Design* (2011)**, definitive monograph, rich on process, film titles and identity programmes.
- **Massimo Vignelli, *The Vignelli Canon* (2010; free PDF)**, a short, opinionated rulebook of modernist discipline.
- **Adrian Shaughnessy, *Herb Lubalin: American Graphic Designer* (2012)**, expressive phototypography and editorial design, well illustrated.
- **Seymour Chwast & Steven Heller (eds.), *The Push Pin Graphic* (2004)**, the full run of the studio's magazine; a catalogue of illustrative and historicist strategies.
- **Bruno Munari, *Design as Art* (1966)**, playful, humane essays on simplicity, signs and objects.
- **Lincoln Cushing, *¡Revolución! Cuban Poster Art* (2003)**, the most accessible survey of ICAIC and OSPAAAL posters with context.
- **Chermayeff, Geismar & Haviv, *Identify* (2011)**, case studies of durable identity marks with the reasoning behind them.

## Archives, museums and collections

- **Museum für Gestaltung Zürich eMuseum** (https://www.emuseum.ch), the largest online collection of Swiss posters; search by designer.
- **Poster Museum at Wilanów, Warsaw** (https://postermuseum.pl/en/about), the world's first poster museum (1968) and home of the Polish school.
- **HfG-Archiv, Museum Ulm** (https://hfg-archiv.museumulm.de), Ulm school, Otl Aicher and Lufthansa/Munich-era systems material.
- **Vignelli Center for Design Studies, RIT** (https://vignellicenter.rit.edu), Vignelli archive; NYC subway, Unigrid and Knoll work.
- **Herb Lubalin Study Center, Cooper Union** (https://lubalincenter.cooper.edu), Lubalin's archive and related New York typography.
- **Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum** (https://collection.cooperhewitt.org), strong American mid-century holdings, searchable online.
- **AIGA Medalists** (https://www.aiga.org/medalists), short, reliable biographies of Rand, Bass, Pineles, Thompson, Wyman, Corita Kent and others.
- **Letterform Archive, San Francisco** (https://letterformarchive.org), type specimens, posters and books from this era, many digitised.
- **Corita Art Center, Los Angeles** (https://www.corita.org), Corita Kent's prints and teaching materials.

## Facsimiles worth owning

- **NYC Transit Authority Graphics Standards Manual (1970; reissue 2014)** and **NASA Graphics Standards Manual (1975; reissue 2015)**, the clearest primary examples of the identity manual as a design system document.
