# Sources, graphic design c.1975 to today

The strongest resources for studying postmodern, digital-era and contemporary graphic design. One line each on why it is worth your time.

## Books

- **Philip B. Meggs & Alston W. Purvis, *Meggs' History of Graphic Design* (5th ed., 2011), ch. 22 to 24**, the standard survey; best single overview of postmodernism, national scenes (UK, Japan, Netherlands, Korea) and the digital shift.
- **Jeremy Aynsley, *A Century of Graphic Design* (2001), "Late Modern and Postmodernism" and "Design in the Digital Era"**, short, well-illustrated entries on each studio and designer; good for quick comparisons.
- **Rick Poynor, *No More Rules: Graphic Design and Postmodernism* (2003)**, the clearest critical account of what postmodern graphic design was trying to do and where it failed.
- **Rick Poynor & Edward Booth-Clibborn, *Typography Now: The Next Wave* (1991)**, the anthology that defined the early-1990s experimental scene (Cranbrook, CalArts, Dutch and British new wave).
- **Wolfgang Weingart, *Typography: My Way to Typography* (Lars Müller, 2000)**, the source of new-wave typography in the designer's own words and work.
- **Katherine & Michael McCoy, *Cranbrook Design: The New Discourse* (1990)**, primary document of "typography as discourse".
- **Ellen Lupton & J. Abbott Miller, *Design Writing Research* (1996)**, essays that explain deconstruction, the designer as author and the politics of form in plain language.
- **Rudy VanderLans & Zuzana Licko, *Emigre: Graphic Design into the Digital Realm* (1993)**, how the first Macintosh generation turned technical limits into a style.
- **David Reinfurt & Robert Wiesenberger, *Muriel Cooper* (MIT Press, 2017)**, the definitive study of the designer who carried print discipline into interactive, three-dimensional information.
- **Rick Poynor, *Jan van Toorn: Critical Practice* (2008)** and ***The Debate* (Crouwel / Van Toorn, 2015)**, the clearest statement of the objective-versus-subjective argument that still shapes design ethics.
- **Peter Hall & Michael Bierut (eds.), *Tibor Kalman: Perverse Optimist* (1998)**, design as social provocation, with the *Colors* years documented.
- **Michael Bierut, *Seventy-Nine Short Essays on Design* (2007)**, the most readable thinking about everyday design practice from inside a large studio.
- ***Karel Martens: Printed Matter / Drukwerk* (Hyphen Press)**, a model of how rigour and play coexist in one body of work.
- **Erik Spiekermann & E. M. Ginger, *Stop Stealing Sheep & Find Out How Type Works* (1993; rev. 2023)**, the best practical introduction to type for people designing interfaces and information.
- **Metahaven, *Uncorporate Identity* (Lars Müller, 2010)**, identity design read as politics; essential for anyone designing for institutions or states.

## Archives, collections and sites

- **Museum für Gestaltung Zürich eMuseum (https://www.emuseum.ch)**: deep holdings of Swiss and new-wave posters, including Weingart's archive.
- **Stedelijk Museum Amsterdam (https://www.stedelijk.nl)**: Crouwel, Van Toorn, Dutch postmodern and contemporary graphic design.
- **MoMA design collection and "Standard Deviations" (2011) typeface acquisitions (https://www.moma.org)**: Emigre-era digital fonts, Irma Boom's books, posters by Tanaka and Greiman.
- **Cooper Hewitt collection (https://collection.cooperhewitt.org)**: American postmodern and digital-era work with good object records.
- **Letterform Archive (https://letterformarchive.org)**: online archive strong on type, lettering and 20th-century graphic ephemera.
- **Eye magazine archive (https://www.eyemagazine.com)**: the best long-form criticism of the period, from Brody and Emigre to Metahaven.
- **Design Observer (https://designobserver.com)**: two decades of critical writing by Bierut, Helfand, Poynor and others.
- **AIGA Eye on Design (https://eyeondesign.aiga.org)**: current profiles and reappraisals (e.g. Eiko Ishioka) of designers outside the canon.
- **Pentagram project archive (https://www.pentagram.com)**: the clearest public record of idea-led identity systems across decades.
- **Dutch Graphic Roots (https://www.dutchgraphicroots.nl)**: biographies and work of Dutch designers from Crouwel to the present.
