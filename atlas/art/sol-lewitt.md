# Sol LeWitt

**Essence:** The concept does the making: write the rule, let others carry it out, and accept every faithful variation as the work.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Instructions are the work | Wall drawings (from 1968) exist as written instructions carried out by draftsmen; every installation differs slightly | Specify designs as rules (tokens, spacing laws, layout constraints) rather than fixed mockups, so implementations across screens differ yet stay faithful; parameterised components |
| G2 · Exhaustive permutation | Incomplete Open Cubes (1974) shows all 122 ways a cube can be incomplete, as models, drawings and photographs | Render the full variant matrix of a component (size × state × theme × density) as one sheet; design icon families by systematic permutation |
| G3 · Four directions, four colors | Early wall drawings layer vertical, horizontal and two diagonal lines in yellow, red, blue and black | A pattern system of 4 line orientations × 4 colors; overlaying orientations encodes combined states (e.g. two active filters) |
| G4 · Open modular lattice | Cube structures keep a fixed ratio between bar and gap; the empty cells matter | Layout lattices with a fixed gutter-to-column ratio where empty cells are intentional and visible, never filled just because they exist |
| G5 · No exceptions | Once the rules exist, execution should be routine | After the system is set, ban one-off overrides: any exception becomes a new documented rule or is rejected |
| G6 · Layered washes | Later wall drawings overlay translucent ink washes to produce new mixed colors | Large background areas built by overlapping 2–3 translucent fills, deriving mixed hues instead of adding new tokens |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** graphite #555555 in early works; ink washes red #C8352E, yellow #F2C22E, blue #275BA6, grey #8A8A8A; later gouache orange #F28A2E, green #3BA55D, purple #6E4CA3.
- **Type:** typed instruction texts → a mono (IBM Plex Mono, Courier Prime) for specs and captions.
- **Form:** straight and arced lines, open cubes, isometric solids, loose scribble fields, stars and bands.
- **Composition:** wall-scale grids and serial matrices; the architecture sets the frame.

## Attitude

In "Paragraphs on Conceptual Art" (Artforum, 1967) LeWitt argued that planning and decisions come first and execution is perfunctory. He was generous: works can be redrawn anywhere, and draftsmen are credited collaborators. That is, almost literally, the philosophy of a design system and of generative design.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `swiss-international`, `minimalism-art`, `gerhard-richter` — systems and chance replacing taste.
- **Productive tension with:** `henri-matisse` — rule-based placement of hand-cut shapes gives organic warmth inside a generative system.
- **Clashes with:** `psychedelic` — arbitrary ornament obscures the legible rule.

## Don't copy

Specific wall drawings and their instruction texts reproduced as artwork, the Incomplete Open Cubes set, Serial Project #1 (ABCD) (1966). The LeWitt estate controls certification and execution of wall drawings.
