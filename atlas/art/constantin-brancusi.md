# Constantin Brâncuși (1876 to 1957)

**Essence:** Reduce a subject until only its essential motion is left, then let material, polish and base do the rest.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · The verb, not the noun | The *Bird in Space* series (from 1923) shows no wings or feathers, only a tapering upward thrust. He sculpted flight, not a bird. | Icons and marks capture the action, not the object: "send" as a single tapering rising form, "sleep" as one settling curve. Test: if a shape still reads after every detail is removed, keep it; if it needs a detail to be understood, rethink the gesture. |
| G2 · The ovoid as first form | Egg-like volumes recur in *Sleeping Muse* (1909 to 10), *Prometheus* (1911) and *The Newborn* (1915): a head reduced to an almost-egg, alive because it is never quite geometric. | Avatars, buttons and containers use superellipse or ovoid geometry with continuous-curvature corners, not perfect circles and plain rounded rectangles. One slightly asymmetric ovoid per screen as the "living" element (the user's own avatar, the primary action). |
| G3 · The base is part of the work | He designed and stacked his own pedestals of wood, stone and plaster, regrouping sculptures and bases until the line between them nearly disappears. | Every hero object (product shot, key metric, featured card) sits on a designed plinth: a stack of 2 to 3 blocks in contrasting surfaces (a dark slab under a light card, a textured band under a smooth one). The plinth is a component variant, not a drop shadow. |
| G4 · Polish as light | Mirror-polished bronze turns form into reflection; the object partly dissolves into its surroundings. | One reflective surface per product: a metallic material with a single specular highlight that shifts ±10% with pointer or tilt; everything else stays matte. Two reflective elements and the effect becomes trim. |
| G5 · One module, endlessly | The *Endless Column* stacks one rhomboidal unit (oak version 1918; the 29 m column at Târgu Jiu, 1937 to 38) so rhythm implies infinity. | Vertical progress, timelines and long-scroll dividers built by repeating one identical module (e.g. a 24×48px rhombus); completed modules fill, pending ones stay outline. No variation inside the stack. |
| G6 · Material decides the finish | After weeks in Rodin's studio (1907) he turned to direct carving: wood stays rough-hewn and blocky, marble and bronze are smoothed to continuity. | Material tokens with fixed finishes: "wood" surfaces always textured and squared, "stone" always matte and soft-cornered, "metal" always polished. A component may not borrow another material's finish. |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** polished bronze ≈ #B08D57, white marble ≈ #EEEAE2, limestone ≈ #D8CDB8, oak ≈ #8B6A45, plaster white ≈ #F4F1EA, studio grey ≈ #9A968E.
- **Type:** none → for his register, a quiet incised serif such as Cormorant Garamond for titles, or Jost Light for a low-contrast sans.
- **Form:** ovoids, tapering blades, stacked rhomboids, rough-hewn wooden blocks, reflective curves.
- **Composition:** objects grouped with their bases like a still life; photographed by the artist himself under controlled studio light.

## Attitude

Brâncuși sought the essence of things rather than their outward appearance, and believed simplicity was reached, not chosen. He controlled every step, carving, polishing, base, arrangement and even the photographs of his work. In 1928 a US court ruled in *Brancusi v. United States* that a bronze *Bird in Space* was art rather than a taxable metal object, a test case for abstraction itself.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `isamu-noguchi` (his assistant in 1927), `kenya-hara`, essential form, quiet material presence.
- **Productive tension with:** `bauhaus`, Bauhaus geometry made organic by Brâncuși's ovoid and polish: interfaces that are systematic but feel carved and warm.
- **Clashes with:** `memphis`, Memphis multiplies pattern and colour; Brâncuși removes everything but one form.

## Don't copy

The silhouettes of *Bird in Space*, *The Kiss*, *Mademoiselle Pogany*, *Sleeping Muse*, *The Newborn* and *Fish*; the *Endless Column* profile; the Târgu Jiu ensemble (*Table of Silence*, *Gate of the Kiss*) as motifs. Take reduction, plinth and finish, not the shapes.
