# Paul Klee

**Essence:** Small, witty signs and softly stepped color, built with a scientist's rigor and a child's freedom.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · The line goes for a walk | His Pedagogical Sketchbook (1925) treats line as an active agent travelling through the plane | One continuous line threads through a page — section divider, progress path, connector — never broken into separate rules; animate stroke offset with scroll |
| G2 · Magic squares | Grids of soft color rectangles stepped gently in hue and value (1920s) | Category color systems laid out as a loose grid of 5–9 muted hues; neighbours differ by at most one value step, so the set reads as a family |
| G3 · Childlike signs, adult rigor | Arrows, fish, moons, eyes, ladders reduced to pictographs, yet carefully balanced | Icon set drawn in a deliberately naive hand (wavering 1.5px line, uneven terminals) but placed on a strict 24px grid; witty double metaphors (an arrow that is also a figure) |
| G4 · Polyphony | Transparent layers act like voices in counterpoint: dots over bands over fields (Ad Parnassum, 1932) | Layer 2–3 independent rhythms — stripe field, dot grid, text column — with transparency so each stays readable; also for layered data series |
| G5 · Textured grounds | He painted on burlap, newsprint, gauze, plaster, and used oil-transfer drawing | Every surface carries faint grain (2–4% noise) and drawings have a slight transfer softness; no pure flat digital white |
| G6 · Titles as part of the work | Poetic titles complete the image and its humor | Microcopy as short poetry: empty states and section names of 2–4 imaginative words, never jokes that block the task |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** terracotta #C1663C, mustard #D8A73A, dusty rose #D98C8C, teal #3E7F7A, night blue #1D2A4A, ochre ground #E7D3A8, brown-black #3A2C24.
- **Type:** hand-drawn letters inside some paintings → a humanist sans (Source Sans 3) with hand-drawn accents; avoid novelty "child" fonts.
- **Form:** arrows, eyes, moons, fish, houses, ladders, mosaics of dots, oil-transfer lines.
- **Composition:** grids, horizontal strata, floating signs on modulated grounds.

## Attitude

Klee believed art should make the invisible visible rather than copy appearances. As a Bauhaus teacher (1921–31) he wrote the most complete artist's course in form, motion and color of the century. His work proves that playfulness and systematic thinking are allies.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `wassily-kandinsky`, `surrealism`, `push-pin-studios` — pictographic wit and color theory.
- **Productive tension with:** `swiss-international` — rigorous typography holding naive drawings creates warm, intelligent editorial design.
- **Clashes with:** `pop-art` — mass-media loudness flattens Klee's private, delicate poetry.

## Don't copy

Twittering Machine (1922), Fish Magic (1925), Ad Parnassum (1932), Senecio (1922), Angelus Novus (1920) — and his specific sign repertoire assembled as a pastiche.
