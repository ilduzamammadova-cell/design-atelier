# Keith Haring

**Essence:** A fast, unbroken line of dancing figures and radiating marks — public, cheap, joyful and politically urgent.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · One confident line, no sketch | He drew directly in a uniform heavy stroke without preparatory drawing | Illustrations in one monoline weight (4–6px at 1x), round caps, flat single-color fills only; drawn in one pass, no construction lines |
| G2 · Motion marks | Short radiating dashes around a figure signal energy, sound, glow | Feedback by radiating tick marks around an icon (success, new, live), animated once for 200–400ms |
| G3 · Allover interlocking figures | Figures fill the whole surface edge to edge with no background left | Celebration screens, packaging and merch patterns where interlocking glyphs or characters leave no empty ground |
| G4 · Made for cheap, public media | Chalk drawings on black paper covering empty subway ad panels (1980–85); the Pop Shop (1986) sold affordable goods | Every asset must work as a one-color line on black and on white, at sticker size and mural size; shareable assets free to reuse |
| G5 · A pictogram language for causes | A small cast (baby, dog, heart, figure, pyramid) recombined into messages | A set of 10–20 figures that combine into scenes to explain flows and campaigns; the same figures across product and advocacy |
| G6 · Slogan inside the drawing | Short block-lettered messages integrated with the figures | Campaign lines of four words or fewer, hand-lettered in caps and drawn into the illustration, not laid over it |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** black #000000, white, red #E4202B, yellow #FFD400, blue #0067B1, green #00A859, pink #F28AB3, orange #F7941D.
- **Type:** his hand-drawn block capitals → Permanent Marker as a display fallback; real hand-lettering preferred.
- **Form:** outlined figures, hearts, radiating lines, crawling babies, barking dogs, UFOs.
- **Composition:** allover friezes and murals, figures packed and interlocked.

## Attitude

Haring believed art belonged to everybody and should meet people in the street, the subway and the shop. His work served AIDS awareness, anti-drug and anti-apartheid causes and children's workshops; he set up his foundation in 1989, the year before he died. For designers: accessibility and joy can carry hard messages.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `pop-art`, `jean-michel-basquiat`, `push-pin-studios` — street energy and public messaging.
- **Productive tension with:** `otl-aicher` — systematic pictograms meet expressive figures; an icon language that is both rigorous and alive.
- **Clashes with:** `agnes-martin` — maximal energy against near-silence; the mix is noise.

## Don't copy

The Radiant Baby, the Barking Dog, Crack Is Wack (1986), Tuttomondo (Pisa, 1989), the Ignorance = Fear poster, and any of his specific figures. The Keith Haring Foundation licenses his imagery.
