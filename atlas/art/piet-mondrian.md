# Piet Mondrian

**Essence:** Dynamic equilibrium, unequal rectangles held in tension by line weight and a few pure colors.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Asymmetric balance | A small saturated block balances a large white field; nothing is centered, yet the whole is at rest | One small saturated element (no more than 5% of area) counterweights a large neutral region; avoid centered symmetric heroes |
| G2 · Lines with rank | Black bars vary in thickness; some stop short of the canvas edge | Dividers in 2 to 3 fixed widths (1, 4, 8px) that encode hierarchy; a divider that ends before the container edge signals continuation or an open group |
| G3 · Three colors, three jobs | Only red, yellow, blue plus white, grey, black | Exactly three accent tokens, each with one job (e.g. red = action, blue = link/info, yellow = highlight); everything else greyscale; no tints of the accents |
| G4 · White is not empty | His whites are layered and differ slightly from each other | Use 2 to 3 off-whites (#F7F5EF, #EEEBE3, #E3E0D8) to separate planes without borders or shadows |
| G5 · Rhythm replaces line | Broadway Boogie Woogie (1942 to 43) breaks lines into small colored squares like traffic and jazz | Timelines, progress and transit-like visualisations made of short colored segments in syncopated rhythm, not uniform bars |
| G6 · Edges run off | Planes continue past the canvas edge, implying an infinite grid | On narrow screens let grid cells crop at the viewport edge to invite horizontal scroll and imply continuity |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** red #D0281F, yellow #F4C21A, blue #1D3D8F, black #111111, whites #F4F1E8 and #E9E6DD, grey #BDBCB5.
- **Type:** none in the paintings. A Mondrian-derived interface wants a quiet grotesque (Work Sans, IBM Plex Sans) so line and color carry the structure.
- **Form:** rectangles, black bars, diamond-oriented (lozenge) canvases.
- **Composition:** irregular modular grid, off-center weight, planes cut by the frame.

## Attitude

Mondrian's Neo-Plasticism sought universal harmony through pure relationships, a model for a balanced society. He removed everything particular, curves, diagonals, modelling, until only relation remained. The transferable idea is balance without symmetry; the surface look is a cliché.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `de-stijl`, `swiss-international`, `alexander-calder` (Calder's 1930 visit to Mondrian's studio pushed him into abstraction).
- **Productive tension with:** `henri-matisse`, hand-cut organic shapes inside a Mondrian proportion system bring warmth to rigor.
- **Clashes with:** `art-nouveau`, whiplash curves against strict orthogonals reads as indecision.

## Don't copy

Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow (1930), Broadway Boogie Woogie (1942 to 43), Victory Boogie Woogie (1942 to 44); the literal red-yellow-blue black-grid pastiche (and derivatives like the 1965 Saint Laurent dress). Take balance and line hierarchy, not the look.
