# Gerhard Richter

**Essence:** Painting that distrusts any single style — chance-ordered color grids, blurred photographs, scraped layers — using systems to get past personal taste.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Chance-arranged color grids | Colour Charts (from 1966) and 4900 Colours (2007); his Cologne Cathedral window (2007) uses 72 colors from historic glass placed by random generator, then corrected where chance made accidental figures | Generative palette walls: choose N tokens from a curated set, assign to a grid by seeded random, then hand-fix accidental shapes; for covers, avatars, campaign art |
| G2 · Blur as distance | Photo-paintings softened with a dry brush until the image wavers | Images blurred (4–12px) when inactive, in background or archival; sharp only when in focus |
| G3 · Squeegee drag | Layers of paint scraped with a large squeegee, revealing what lies beneath | Hero textures made by dragging layered color; transitions that drag one layer aside to reveal the next |
| G4 · Grey as position | Grey paintings as a refusal to make a statement | Greyscale-first interfaces where color appears only for data or state |
| G5 · Stripes from images | Strip works (2011) slice an image digitally into thin horizontal lines | Header and loader bars generated by sampling a brand photograph into 1–4px horizontal stripes |
| G6 · The archive as work | Atlas: thousands of source photos, sketches and collages on gridded panels | Moodboard and archive views of many small images on grid panels, where the index itself is presentable |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** greys #8A8A88 and #4D4D4B; squeegee colors alizarin #B11E2F, cadmium yellow #F2C200, phthalo green #1F6B54, ultramarine #283C8F, white streaks; color charts span the spectrum.
- **Type:** none; a neutral grotesque (Schibsted Grotesk) that adds no opinion.
- **Form:** rectangles of color, blurred figures, scraped streaks, stripes, glass panes and mirrors.
- **Composition:** grids for the charts, allover fields for abstractions, photographic framing for figurative work.

## Attitude

Richter has long rejected ideology and the idea of a signature style, moving between photo-based painting and abstraction. Chance and systems let him make images he could not have chosen. For designers: build procedures that surprise you, then edit their output.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `sol-lewitt`, `ellsworth-kelly`, `josef-albers` — systems and chance in color.
- **Productive tension with:** `swiss-international` — blurred atmospheric photographs inside a razor-sharp grid.
- **Clashes with:** `keith-haring` — cool, skeptical ambiguity against exuberant certainty.

## Don't copy

Betty (1988), the Candle paintings (1982–83), Ema (Nude on a Staircase) (1966), 4900 Colours, the Cologne Cathedral window, Birkenau (2014), and specific abstract paintings.
