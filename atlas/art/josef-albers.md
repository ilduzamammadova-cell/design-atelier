# Josef Albers

**Essence:** Color is the most relative medium, the same hue changes with every neighbour, so color must be tested in context, not chosen in isolation.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Interaction of color | Interaction of Color (1963) shows one color reading as two, and two as one, depending on surroundings | Every accent token is tested against every surface it can sit on (light, dark, elevated, tinted); docs show each accent on all surfaces, and on-dark accents get their own values |
| G2 · Nested squares, lowered | Homage to the Square (1950 to 76): three or four squares nested, the inner ones set below center | Concentric containers (frame → panel → card) in stepped tones of one hue, with more space above than below for a grounded feel |
| G3 · Experiment before theory | Students worked with sheets of colored paper, not color wheels or formulas | Palette decisions made from live side-by-side tests inside real components; HSL math only as a starting point |
| G4 · Minimum means, maximum effect | His preliminary course measured results against economy of material | Constrain a product to three colors and one typeface, then get all variety from proportion, value and placement |
| G5 · Documented materials | He recorded the paints used on the back of each panel, applied mostly straight from the tube | Treat tokens as a public recipe: each color token documents its source value, use and the pairings it was tested against |
| G6 · Modular letters | In the 1920s he drew a stencil alphabet assembled from a few geometric units | Display lettering or icons constructed from 3 to 4 modular parts (square, quarter circle, bar) on a unit grid |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** families of close relations: yellow #E8C547, ochre #C88B2C, warm grey #8F8E88, oxide red #A23A2C, olive #7A7A3A, mid blue #4F6FA5.
- **Type:** his geometric stencil alphabet → for UI a geometric sans (Jost) or a modular stencil for display only.
- **Form:** squares in squares, matte paint applied with a palette knife on board, glass assemblages, line constellations.
- **Composition:** centered horizontally, lowered vertically; strict proportional steps between bands.

## Attitude

Albers was above all a teacher, Bauhaus (from 1923), Black Mountain College (1933 to 49), Yale (1950 to 58). His aim was to train perception: seeing is learned, and design begins with noticing how things act on each other. Systems thinkers can treat his method as a test suite for color.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `bauhaus`, `anni-albers`, `op-art` (his students carried his color exercises into optical painting).
- **Productive tension with:** `henri-matisse`, controlled nested color meets hand-cut joy; precision with warmth.
- **Clashes with:** `psychedelic`, uncontrolled vibrating color dissolves the precise relational logic.

## Don't copy

Specific Homage to the Square paintings and their exact color sets; the plate compositions of Interaction of Color; Structural Constellations drawings; his stencil alphabet.
