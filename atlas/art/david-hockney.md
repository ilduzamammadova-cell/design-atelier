# David Hockney

**Essence:** A lifelong pleasure in looking — flat sunlit color, water drawn as line, many viewpoints stitched together, and every new tool taken up with curiosity.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Flat, hard light | The Los Angeles paintings use flat acrylic planes, crisp architecture, sharp shadows | Illustration and photo direction: flat color planes with hard shadows at one consistent sun angle across all assets |
| G2 · Water as calligraphy | Pool surfaces become wiggly lines on blue; A Bigger Splash (1967) freezes one burst in a still scene | Animated wavy-line patterns in 2–3 blues for water or leisure surfaces; one moment of motion in an otherwise static layout |
| G3 · Joiners | From 1982, collages of many photographs taken from shifting positions (Pearblossom Hwy., 1986) | Galleries as overlapping mosaics of snapshots at slight rotations building one scene — ideal for trip or event recaps |
| G4 · Use the tool's own marks | Fax, photocopier, iPhone and iPad drawings; the Queen's Window at Westminster Abbey (2018) was drawn on an iPad | Let digital brushes look digital: stylus strokes, layered transparent swipes; don't fake oil paint |
| G5 · Reverse and moving perspective | Landscapes where roads widen as they recede and viewpoints shift | Illustrated maps and onboarding scenes with reverse or multi-point perspective to show more of the path at once |
| G6 · Non-natural complements | Yorkshire landscapes with violet roads and orange trees | Seasonal themes built from bold complements (violet path, orange foliage) to give ordinary scenes energy |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** pool blue #2BA6D8, sky cyan #7EC8E3, stucco pink #F2A7B0, palm green #3D8B4F, mustard #E0B13A, violet #7B5EA7, terracotta #D2693C.
- **Type:** occasional hand-lettered titles; for UI a friendly neutral sans (DM Sans) that stays out of the color's way.
- **Form:** pools, palms, modernist houses, splashes, chairs, trees, roads.
- **Composition:** frontal, stage-like (he designed opera sets), horizontal bands, collaged grids.

## Attitude

Hockney distrusts the single fixed eye of the camera and of one-point perspective; seeing takes time and movement. He treats each new technology as a drawing instrument and the act of looking as joy. For designers: optimism and curiosity about tools are a style in themselves.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `henri-matisse` (the Abbey window's flat color draws on Matisse), `pop-art`, `mid-century-modern`, `cubism` (joiners extend multi-view seeing).
- **Productive tension with:** `minimalism-art` — sunlit color on strict boxy forms: modernist architecture made cheerful.
- **Clashes with:** `punk-graphics` — sunny optimism and photocopied aggression cancel each other.

## Don't copy

A Bigger Splash (1967), Portrait of an Artist (Pool with Two Figures) (1972), Mr and Mrs Clark and Percy (1970–71), Pearblossom Hwy. (1986), Bigger Trees Near Warter (2007), The Queen's Window (2018).
