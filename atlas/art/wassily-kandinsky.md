# Wassily Kandinsky

**Essence:** Color and form treated like sound — elements with inner character, composed the way music is composed.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Color–form correspondence | At the Bauhaus (1923 questionnaire) he linked yellow to triangle, red to square, blue to circle | Bind shape and color to meaning together: status chips where shape encodes category (triangle = warning, square = stable, circle = info) and color reinforces it, so meaning survives color blindness |
| G2 · Point, line, plane | Point and Line to Plane (1926) treats all composition as three primitives with tensions between them | Diagrams and illustrations built from exactly three primitives: dots (6–12px nodes), lines (1–3px, straight or a single arc), flat planes; a coherent vocabulary for flows and data |
| G3 · Visual music | Works titled Improvisation, Impression, Composition; color as a keyboard acting on the soul | Map sound to sight in audio products: pitch → hue, loudness → size, tempo → easing; generative visuals that respond to rhythm |
| G4 · Circles on a dark field | Several Circles (1926) floats overlapping translucent discs in a deep ground | Dark mode with 3–9 translucent circular color fields (multiply or screen blending) as ambient background, never under body text |
| G5 · Temperature makes depth | Warm yellows advance toward the viewer, cool blues recede | Give the element that must come forward (primary action) a warm hue and push chrome back with cool hues; depth from temperature instead of shadow |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** cadmium yellow #F2C500, vermilion #E03C31, ultramarine #2341A0, violet #6B3FA0, black, cream ground #EFE6D2, night #141826.
- **Type:** no typefaces of his own; a Bauhaus-era geometric sans fits (Jost, Futura-like).
- **Form:** circles, triangles, checkerboards, arcs, wavy and zigzag lines, small grids.
- **Composition:** strong diagonals, elements floating in a field, centrifugal clusters; early works lyrical and organic, Bauhaus works geometric.

## Attitude

Kandinsky argued that painting could be as nonobjective as music and speak directly to emotion. He was also a systematic teacher (Bauhaus, 1922–33) who wanted students to analyse every element. The combination — feeling plus taxonomy — is what transfers to design systems.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `paul-klee`, `bauhaus`, `sonia-delaunay` — color theory as both expression and pedagogy.
- **Productive tension with:** `sol-lewitt` — explicit rules meet musical improvisation; good ground for generative, sound-reactive visuals.
- **Clashes with:** `minimalism-art` — Judd rejected composition itself; Kandinsky's orchestration on serial objects reads as arbitrary decoration.

## Don't copy

Composition VIII (1923), Several Circles (1926), Yellow-Red-Blue (1925), Composition VII (1913), the Blue Rider almanac cover (1912).
