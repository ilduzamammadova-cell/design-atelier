# Giorgio Morandi

**Essence:** A handful of humble objects, painted again and again in greyed, dusty tones whose values sit almost on top of each other.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Dusty mid-tones, tiny steps | Ochre-grey, powdered pink, putty and bone, all within a narrow band of lightness | A neutral palette of 6 to 8 greyed tones with only 3 to 6 L* between neighbours; surfaces differ by temperature, not value; one darker tone reserved for text (checked at 4.5:1) |
| G2 · Few objects, endless arrangements | The same bottles, boxes and jugs recombined for decades | A fixed kit of 5 to 7 container shapes recombined for marketing art, empty states and illustrations; constraint becomes identity |
| G3 · Objects that touch | Forms huddle and overlap; contours merge where they meet | Product images or cards grouped with zero or negative gaps (4 to 12px overlap), read as one family; edges soften where they meet |
| G4 · Kill the reflection | Studio accounts describe labels removed and glass painted over so nothing glinted | Matte everything: no specular highlights, no glossy renders; de-branded product photography for editorial calm |
| G5 · Low, frontal ledge | Objects sit on a shallow table seen almost straight on | Product photography and 3D at a low, frontal angle on one horizontal ledge aligned to a layout baseline |
| G6 · Tone by hatching | His etchings build tone from fine cross-hatching | Illustration shading by fine hatch patterns rather than gradients or blur |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** putty #CFC4B0, bone #E8E0CF, dusty pink #D3B5A4, ochre grey #B7A27F, stone #9C978C, umber #7B6A57, blue-grey #A9B0B2.
- **Type:** none; a quiet old-style serif (Libre Caslon Text, Cormorant) or a low-contrast humanist sans, small and loosely spaced.
- **Form:** bottles, boxes, tins, jugs; soft edges, visible brush, a little imprecision in verticals.
- **Composition:** frontal cluster near the center, broad flat background, a single table edge as horizon.

## Attitude

Morandi spent his life in one modest room on Via Fondazza in Bologna, looking at the same things with complete attention. Stillness and repetition were his research method. For designers: depth comes from narrowing the field, not widening it.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `agnes-martin`, `japanese-wabi-sabi-and-ma`, `kenya-hara`, `kenya-hara`, quiet, greyed, attentive.
- **Productive tension with:** `ellsworth-kelly`, bold hard-edged silhouettes in a dusty Morandi palette give a new "muted hard-edge".
- **Clashes with:** `op-art`, maximum contrast and vibration against minimum contrast; nothing survives.

## Don't copy

His specific still lifes and etchings; a literal cluster of dusty bottles used as a brand motif.
