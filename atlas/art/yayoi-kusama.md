# Yayoi Kusama

**Essence:** One motif repeated until object, body and room dissolve into it — repetition as both compulsion and cosmos.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Obliteration by repetition | Dots and nets cover objects, bodies and rooms until boundaries disappear | One motif applied across every surface — product, packaging, avatar, venue — with density rising as a signal (e.g. more marks = more activity) |
| G2 · Infinity by mirrors | Infinity Mirror Room – Phalli's Field (1965) multiplied forms endlessly with mirrors | Recursion as experience: infinite-zoom canvases, nested reflections, many small light points receding on dark backgrounds |
| G3 · The net | Infinity Net paintings (late 1950s) repeat tiny looping strokes across huge canvases | Low-contrast background texture of tiny hand-made loops, high-resolution so obsessive detail rewards zoom |
| G4 · Audience completes the surface | In the Obliteration Room (from 2002) visitors cover a white room with colored dot stickers | Shared surfaces where each user action leaves a visible mark that accumulates (collaborative canvases, community maps) |
| G5 · Strict two-color pairs | Red with white, yellow with black: motif plus ground only | Each product line or campaign gets exactly one motif color and one ground color; no third color in the pattern |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** red #E60012, white #FFFFFF, yellow #F7D000, black #000000; nets in white over black or red.
- **Type:** not part of the work; type should be a calm rounded sans on solid ground, never on the pattern.
- **Form:** dots of many sizes, looping nets, soft stuffed forms, pumpkins, mirrors, suspended lights.
- **Composition:** allover, edge to edge, environments that surround the viewer.

## Attitude

Kusama describes her work as rooted in hallucination and in the wish to obliterate the self into the universe. She embraced performance, fashion and mass culture early, and later large brand collaborations. For designers: a single obsessive gesture can carry a whole identity if it is pursued without compromise.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `pop-art`, `op-art`, `keith-haring` — allover repetition and public spectacle.
- **Productive tension with:** `agnes-martin` — both repeat tiny marks over grids and nets; quiet versus obsessive gives meditative but rich textures.
- **Clashes with:** `dieter-rams` — total repetition and excess against less-but-better.

## Don't copy

Infinity Mirror Rooms (Phalli's Field, 1965; The Souls of Millions of Light Years Away, 2013), Pumpkin (Naoshima, 1994), the Louis Vuitton collaborations (2012, 2023), her persona and image. A field of multi-size red-on-white dots covering an object reads as hers — avoid it.
