# Agnes Martin

**Essence:** A hand-ruled pencil grid over pale washes — perfection approached, never reached, at the threshold of visibility.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · The breathing grid | Lines ruled in pencil on a gessoed ground waver slightly; the grid feels drawn, not printed | Background grid lines at 1px and 6–10% opacity with slight irregularity (varied opacity or 0.5px offsets) so the structure feels human |
| G2 · A highly divisible module | She chose a 72-inch square because 72 divides so many ways, later working at 60 inches | Pick a base unit divisible by 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 and 12 (e.g. 24 or 72px) so columns, gutters and type rhythm share one modulus |
| G3 · Pale horizontal bands | Washes of faint pink, blue and cream in horizontal stripes, separated by pencil lines | Long-form reading and tables in alternating near-identical tints (#F6F1EA / #EEF1F2) with hairline separators instead of cards |
| G4 · Detail reveals on approach | From afar the canvas looks blank; close up the lines appear | Progressive disclosure by proximity: secondary labels and guides appear on hover, focus or zoom; essential text keeps full contrast at all times |
| G5 · Emptying out | No image, no gesture, no ego | Remove every non-essential element: one action per screen, no illustration, small type and space doing all the work |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** gesso white #F4F1EA, pale pink #EED9D3, pale blue #D9E4EA, cream #F2E8CF, pale grey #DADAD5, graphite #6E6E6A.
- **Type:** none; for UI a light humanist serif or sans at small sizes (Source Serif 4 Light, Hanken Grotesk Light at 13–14px), generously leaded.
- **Form:** ruled lines, bands, rectangles; square canvases.
- **Composition:** allover and frontal, a narrow unpainted margin at the edges, no focal point.

## Attitude

Martin described her paintings as being about innocence, happiness and perfection as states of mind, not about nature. She left New York in 1967, stopped painting for several years, and resumed in New Mexico in the 1970s. For a designer the lesson is subtraction as an act of care, and patience as method.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `kenya-hara`, `kenya-hara`, `mark-rothko`, `anni-albers` — quiet structure and emptiness.
- **Productive tension with:** `hilma-af-klint` — both sincere and spiritual; her emptiness against af Klint's diagrams yields calm but symbolic interfaces.
- **Clashes with:** `memphis` — loud, ironic pattern annihilates the threshold-level calm.

## Don't copy

Friendship (1963, gold leaf), The Islands I–XII (1979), Night Sea (1963), With My Back to the World (1997), and any specific stripe sequence or grid measurement taken from a named painting.
