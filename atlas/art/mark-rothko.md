# Mark Rothko

**Essence:** Soft-edged fields of close-valued color, large enough to stand inside, built from thin glowing layers to carry emotion directly.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Stacked soft fields | Two or three rectangles float on a colored ground, edges feathered | Section backgrounds as stacked horizontal color bands whose edges blend over 24 to 64px; content floats inside each band, never across a blend |
| G2 · Close-value chords | Red on maroon, orange on red: hues differ, values nearly match | Surfaces and elevations separated by hue shift at near-equal value; text always uses a separate high-contrast token (4.5:1 minimum) |
| G3 · Immersion by scale and nearness | Large canvases meant to be seen close, in low light | Full-bleed color filling the viewport in focus or listening modes; chrome hidden, ambient UI dimmed |
| G4 · Glazed depth | Many thin translucent layers make the color seem lit from within | Build surfaces from 2 to 3 stacked translucent fills (layered CSS gradients) rather than one flat hex, giving an inner glow |
| G5 · The room is the work | The Rothko Chapel, Houston (opened 1971) sets fourteen paintings in an octagon with controlled daylight | Design whole sequences (onboarding, a session, a ritual) where color and light change slowly in stages and nothing competes |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** cadmium red #C8341E, maroon #5E1A1D, orange #E07A2E, saffron #E8A93A, plum #4B2C3F, deep blue #23324D, near-black #1E1A1D.
- **Type:** none; type must recede, small, light serif or sans (EB Garamond, Hanken Grotesk Light) placed at the edge of the field.
- **Form:** rectangles with dissolving edges; stain-like thin paint.
- **Composition:** vertical canvas, symmetrical stacking, a margin of ground around every field.

## Attitude

Rothko insisted he was not a colorist: the paintings were about basic human emotions, tragedy, ecstasy, doom, and an intimate encounter with the viewer. He controlled hanging, lighting and distance. For designers: color can be an emotional environment, not a decoration added to one.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `agnes-martin`, `james-turrell`, `hilma-af-klint`, color as a space for contemplation.
- **Productive tension with:** `josef-albers`, hard-edged nested color logic against soft-edged feeling; gives calm, precise color systems.
- **Clashes with:** `pop-art`, ironic glossy surface destroys the sincerity the fields require.

## Don't copy

The Seagram Murals (1958 to 59), the Rothko Chapel paintings, No. 61 (Rust and Blue) (1953), and the exact color pairings of named canvases. Generic "Rothko gradient" heroes are a cliché.

## Study

- National Gallery of Art, Washington, largest public Rothko collection (nga.gov)
