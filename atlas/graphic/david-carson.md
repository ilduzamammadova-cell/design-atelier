# David Carson

**Essence:** Layout as improvisation: each spread responds to how the article feels, even when that costs conventional legibility.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Read first, then react | No templates: at *Transworld Skateboarding*, *Beach Culture* and *Ray Gun* each page was designed after reading the text and responding to its mood. | Long-form features get a custom art-direction slot (hero layout and headline treatment per article), while navigation, body measure and controls stay templated and identical. |
| G2 · Promote the minor element | Page numbers set huge, captions turned into display type, pull quotes as structure. | In a feature, one normally tiny element (section number, caption, timestamp) is set at display size (≥64px) as the opening graphic. |
| G3 · Cinematic continuity | Headlines and images flow across spreads and wrap around page edges, like cuts in film. | Scroll-linked story: headline fragments continue across viewport sections and images bleed from one section into the next (negative margins); sequence follows the scroll. |
| G4 · Expressive layer, clean twin | He argued that legibility is not the same as communication, and once set a dull interview entirely in a dingbat font. | Distorted or overlapping text lives only in decorative layers marked `aria-hidden`, always paired with a clean, WCAG-compliant version of the same words nearby. |
| G5 · Unnatural crop | Faces cut at the eyes, letters sliced away, images cropped to unsettle. | Editorial images use per-image `object-position` chosen by hand; crops may cut subjects at unexpected points. Never auto-centre in feature heroes. |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** photographic and murky, with fluorescent accents; type in low contrast over images.
- **Type:** distressed and experimental 1990s faces (Emigre-era), typewriter, grotesques stretched or sliced → free: Special Elite, Archivo Narrow, Bricolage Grotesque.
- **Form:** overlapping columns, gutterless text, reversed leading, slashed letters, smeared photography.
- **Composition:** no grid; each spread an event; motion implied by overlap and blur.

## Attitude

A former sociology teacher and professional surfer, Carson trusted intuition over rules and treated design as personal expression. He believed readers who care will work harder than designers assume, and that dense, layered pages can attract people that tidy ones lose. His approach moved from niche magazines to global advertisers within a decade.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `punk-graphics`, `emigre` — DIY energy and experimental digital type.
- **Productive tension with:** `swiss-international` — one Carson spread per issue inside a strict Swiss grid gives controlled shock.
- **Clashes with:** `wim-crouwel` — zero order meets total order; the result is noise, not tension.

## Don't copy

*Ray Gun* covers and spreads (including the dingbat-set interview), the *Beach Culture* "hanging type" spread, the *Surfer* covers, the Nine Inch Nails *The Fragile* graphics.
