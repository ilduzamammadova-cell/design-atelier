# Dan Friedman

**Essence:** "Radical modernism": keep modernism's idealism and systems, then let decoration, pop culture and fantasy back in.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Legibility vs readability | He separated efficient decoding (legibility) from the pleasure and challenge of reading (readability) and asked students to serve both. | Two type modes in the system: `legible` for chrome and forms (16/24, neutral sans, no effects) and `readable` for editorial headlines (scale, weight and spacing play allowed). Never mix modes inside one component. |
| G2 · One plain text, many operations | His Yale exercises ran a dull weather report through ordered operations: position, weight, slant, spacing, clustering. | Generate component variants by changing exactly one variable at a time from a fixed content sample; keep the variant matrix in the library so every option is traceable. |
| G3 · System plus one wild layer | A clean corporate mark (Citibank, with Anspach Grossman Portugal) and, elsewhere, loose hand-drawn forms layered over mechanical patterns. | Base layout fully systematic; one decorative layer (hand-drawn SVG pattern) sits behind content, has no interactive role, and can be toggled off by theme. |
| G4 · Cheap and noble together | Folding screens of painted fibreboard, feathers and drink cans; folk and industrial materials as equals. | Pair precise typography with one "vernacular" texture token (laminate pattern, clip-art, packing paper) on brand surfaces; swapping the token must not change layout. |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** Memphis-adjacent brights — cadmium red `#D7262B`, cobalt `#1F4FB5`, yellow `#F6C915`, black — alongside very plain white typographic work.
- **Type:** Univers 55 and 65 for exercises, Franklin Gothic white on black, bold geometric logos → free: Libre Franklin, Familjen Grotesk.
- **Form:** squiggles, chevrons, stripes and spots; painted cut-out furniture shapes.
- **Composition:** structured base with spontaneous gestures crossing it; his own apartment was a full-scale test of the idea.

## Attitude

Friedman rejected the claim that modernism was dead; he wanted it widened to include more culture, history, research and fantasy. He moved between corporate identity, furniture, art and issue-driven publishing (AIDS, nuclear war) without ranking them. Design for him was a way of looking at meaning, not only at efficiency.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `memphis`, `pop-art` — shared delight in laminate pattern and popular imagery.
- **Productive tension with:** `massimo-vignelli` — the same corporate discipline, opposite views on decoration; a Vignelli grid with one Friedman layer stays orderly but alive.
- **Clashes with:** `dieter-rams` — a decorative layer next to "less but better" products reads as costume.

## Don't copy

The 1971 Typografische Monatsblätter cover, the Citibank logo, the Driade folding screens, the Bonwit Teller packaging proposal, his apartment interior.
