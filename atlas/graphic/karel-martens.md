# Karel Martens

**Essence:** Play inside rules: overlapping coloured circles and found machine parts printed by hand, grids that leave room for chance.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Prints from found parts | His monoprints press Meccano pieces, washers and other found metal objects onto discarded index cards and packing paper, overprinting the same sheet for years. | A generative pattern library of a few geometric "parts" (rings, slotted strips, discs) placed by seeded randomness and overprinted with `multiply`; the seed changes per page, the parts never do. |
| G2 · Colour from overlap | Transparent inks layered so new colours appear only where shapes cross. | Three transparent ink tokens (cyan, magenta, yellow-ish) with `mix-blend-mode: multiply`; any extra colour must come from overlaps, never from a new token. Text never sits on overlaps. |
| G3 · Rule plus margin for chance | Strict modular rules with a controlled element of accident, so each print or cover is a variation, not a copy. | Seeded layouts: positions jitter ±10% inside fixed grid cells; designers choose and save seeds they like, so "chance" stays reproducible. |
| G4 · Fixed format, changing experiment | As art director of the architecture journal *OASE* (from 1990), he kept a constant frame while each issue tested a new typographic idea, often with students. | Editorial channel with a fixed format (size, masthead position, body type) and one new typographic experiment per issue, documented in a public log. |
| G5 · Text at the scale of a building | Typographic façades (e.g. the Veenman printing works in Ede, 1996) repeat letters as a rhythmic pattern legible up close. | A long background band of repeated characters or text along a page edge, legible locally, read as pattern globally; purely decorative and `aria-hidden`. |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** process cyan, magenta and yellow overprints, warm pinks and oranges, on beige, grey and buff recycled card.
- **Type:** sober grotesques and clear book typography; letters as rhythmic modules on façades → free: Schibsted Grotesk, Public Sans, IBM Plex Sans.
- **Form:** circles, rings, slotted strips, gears, dot grids; the impression of real objects.
- **Composition:** modular, repetitive, layered; quiet structure with playful accident.

## Attitude

Martens works modestly and constantly, treating making as a form of thinking; much of his best work is self-initiated printing done almost daily on a small press. He believes in learning by doing: with Wigger Bierma he founded the Werkplaats Typografie in Arnhem (1998), where teachers and students take real commissions side by side.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `irma-boom`, `experimental-jetset`, Dutch book craft and modernist rigour with wit.
- **Productive tension with:** `wim-crouwel`, the same module, but loosened by chance and colour mixing.
- **Clashes with:** `david-carson`, both play, but Carson discards the rule that makes Martens' chance meaningful; together the play loses its point.

## Don't copy

Specific monoprints, *OASE* covers, the Veenman façade, his Dutch postage stamps, the cover of *Printed Matter / Drukwerk*.
