# Experimental Jetset

**Essence:** Modernism as a mother tongue spoken with post-punk attitude: Helvetica, primary contrast and grids used to make the medium itself visible.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · The list as image | The "John & Paul & Ringo & George" T-shirt (2001) replaced a band photo with a plain list of first names in Helvetica. | Replace a hero image with a large typographic list of the actual contents (names, features, contributors) in one grotesque, with ampersands as rhythm. |
| G2 · Responsive mark | The Whitney Museum identity (2013) is a zigzag "W" line that stretches to fit any format and acts as a grid for text and images. | Logo as a single-weight line that adapts to container proportions and holds content between its segments; one stroke-weight token, no fixed lockup. |
| G3 · Show the medium | Their work tends to expose its own construction: format, fold, printing, grid. | Expose structural truths in the UI: scroll progress, page boundaries in reading views, the grid as visible rules; nothing pretends to be something else. |
| G4 · Neutral type, firm intent | A small set of neo-grotesques (Helvetica, Neue Haas Grotesk) is treated as a zero point that lets structure carry meaning. | One grotesque in two weights; hierarchy only through size and position. No italics, no coloured text, no decorative type. |
| G5 · Primary blocks | Black, white and one primary colour, often as full-width bands. | Palette limited to black, white and one primary (red or blue); colour appears only as full-width horizontal bands or solid blocks, never as tints. |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** black, white, signal red `#E4002B` or cobalt `#0033A0`, used flat.
- **Type:** Helvetica, Neue Haas Grotesk, occasionally Univers or custom geometric lettering → free: Hanken Grotesk, Schibsted Grotesk, Public Sans.
- **Form:** lines, bars, ampersands, lists, grids, folded formats.
- **Composition:** rigid grids, big type, flat colour fields; humour comes from the concept, not decoration.

## Attitude

Founded in 1997 by Marieke Stolk, Erwin Brinkers and Danny van den Dungen, the studio calls modernism its mother tongue, inherited from Wim Crouwel and Benno Wissing, and mixes it with the DIY spirit of 1980s post-punk. They see modernism as a critical, materialist project rather than a style, and write extensively about design as a way of making the world visible as constructed.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `wim-crouwel`, `michael-bierut`, systematic modernism with dry wit.
- **Productive tension with:** `stefan-sagmeister`, one handmade, personal element in a rigorous grid.
- **Clashes with:** `hard-werken`, radical consistency and radical eclecticism cancel each other.

## Don't copy

The "John & Paul & Ringo & George" shirt and its variants, the Whitney "Responsive W", the Stedelijk Museum identity they designed, the *Helvetica* film poster ("Meet the Cast", 2006).
