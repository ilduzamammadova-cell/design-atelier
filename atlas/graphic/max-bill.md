# Max Bill (1908–1994)

**Essence:** Art and design built from mathematical thinking — concrete elements, exact proportions and lowercase calm.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Concrete element | Forms that depict nothing — planes, colours, lines — whose proportions come from arithmetic, so the composition is its own subject. | Replace decorative illustration with "concrete" shapes whose sizes are derived from the layout math (e.g. a square exactly 3 × 3 modules, a bar 1 × 8). No shape exists without a numeric reason. |
| G2 · Single case | Bill favoured lowercase-only typography; one case lowers the voice and unifies the line. | Navigation, labels, wordmark and section titles in lowercase (proper nouns optional); sentence capitals only in long reading text. |
| G3 · Progression and permutation | Arithmetic and geometric series, rotations and permutations generate variety from one rule. | Spacing and size scales as explicit series (arithmetic 8/16/24/32 for spacing, geometric 1.25 for type); pattern backgrounds generated by permuting one motif through rotations. |
| G4 · Space instead of indent | In his book work paragraphs are separated by an interval of space rather than indented, and lines run ragged right. | Paragraph spacing = one line-height, no first-line indents, ragged right; lists separated by space, not bullets or rules. |
| G5 · Hanging axis | Asymmetric pages whose type hangs from a single vertical axis near the centre, achieving order without symmetry. | Long-form pages with a vertical axis at 5/12: headings start on the axis, metadata hangs to its left flush right, body text runs right of it. |
| G6 · One fluid container | His 1930s Wohnbedarf poster gathers unrelated products inside one organic red shape; a single outline unifies variety. | Group heterogeneous items (products, features) inside one continuous soft-edged background shape rather than separate cards. |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** white, black, pure primaries and secondaries in precise areas; red used structurally.
- **Type:** Akzidenz-Grotesk, especially the medium weight, lowercase → Archivo Medium, Schibsted Grotesk Medium.
- **Form:** squares on the diagonal, circles, rotated squares, organic blobs in early work.
- **Composition:** diamonds and wedges of photographs, central axes, arithmetic subdivisions.

## Attitude

A Bauhaus graduate who preferred to call himself an architect, Bill believed design should make everyday life responsible and good, and that mathematical order was a humane, universal language. As first rector of the Ulm school (1953–56) he carried that belief into postwar design education.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `karl-gerstner`, `ulm-school` — rule-based form and systematic education.
- **Productive tension with:** `bruno-munari` — fellow concrete-art pioneer; Bill's exact proportions plus Munari's play gives systematic yet joyful interfaces.
- **Clashes with:** `art-nouveau` — whiplash ornament has no arithmetic to hold onto.

## Don't copy

*zeitprobleme in der schweizer malerei und plastik* (1936), *USA baut* (1945), the Wohnbedarf posters, *konkrete kunst* posters, his Junghans clock faces (product).
