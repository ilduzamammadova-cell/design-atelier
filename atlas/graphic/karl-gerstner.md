# Karl Gerstner (1930 to 2017)

**Essence:** Design the rule set, not the result, creativity as choosing among systematically generated possibilities.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Design as programme | *Designing Programmes* (1964) argues that a solution should be described as a set of rules that can produce many correct outcomes; the designer selects from the space they define. | Build components as parameter matrices (size × density × emphasis × tone) and generate every combination in a story or Figma variant grid; review the whole space, then lock the allowed subset. |
| G2 · Mobile grid | The grid he built for *Capital* magazine (early 1960s) overlays several column systems so one page can resolve into 2, 3, 4, 5 or 6 columns while sharing edges. | Use a container width divisible by 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 (e.g. 60 units of 20px = 1200px). Layout primitives accept `cols={2..6}` and always land on shared unit lines. |
| G3 · Morphological box | Ideas are found by listing parameters and their possible values, then combining them, invention as a table. | Style-generation workshops use a table: rows = parameters (type, colour, shape, motion), columns = values; generate combinations procedurally before sketching. |
| G4 · Integral typography | The form of a word should grow from its meaning or structure, so the logotype is derived by a rule rather than decorated. | Derive the wordmark by one explicit rule (e.g. one character shifted a baseline, or one weight step), then reuse that rule as a UI motif (active tab shifts the same amount). |
| G5 · Colour as ordered series | His paintings explore colour as systematic progressions and permutations rather than taste. | Generate colour ramps in OKLCH with equal lightness steps; use them as ordered sequences for charts and states, never picked ad hoc. |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** black, white and primaries in advertising; in art, full spectral progressions arranged in steps.
- **Type:** Akzidenz-Grotesk; his own Gerstner-Programm (1964 to 67, Berthold filmsetting) → Archivo, Schibsted Grotesk; Gerstner-Programm digital revival (2017).
- **Form:** modular grids, permutational patterns, concentric and stepped forms in art.
- **Composition:** grid-based, information-dense, logic visible in the arrangement.

## Attitude

Gerstner, a founder of the agency GGK, believed that a method could be both rigorous and inventive, that describing the problem precisely was itself creative work. His thinking anticipates parametric and computational design: the designer as author of a system that others, or machines, can run.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `max-bill`, `otl-aicher`, arithmetic order extended into rule-based systems.
- **Productive tension with:** `massin`, a programme that generates expressive, literary typography: variable-font systems where voice is a parameter.
- **Clashes with:** `arts-and-crafts`, the one-off handmade object is the opposite of a generator.

## Don't copy

The exact *Capital* grid diagram, his advertising campaigns and logotypes for GGK clients, his *Color Sounds* and other artworks.

## Study

- *Compendium for Literates* (*Kompendium für Alphabeten*, 1972).
