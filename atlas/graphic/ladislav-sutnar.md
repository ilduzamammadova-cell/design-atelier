# Ladislav Sutnar (1897–1976)

**Essence:** Information design before the name — function, flow and form arranged so readers find, scan and understand at speed.

In Prague he was design director of the cooperative publisher Družstevní práce and also designed toys, tableware and textiles. He came to New York for the Czechoslovak pavilion of the 1939 World's Fair and stayed when his country was occupied. With research director Knut Lönberg-Holm he systematised Sweet's Catalog Service (1941–60) and wrote *Catalog Design* (1944) and *Catalog Design Progress* (1950). He is widely credited with the parentheses around US telephone area codes for the Bell System.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Function → flow → form | Information must be easy to find, read, understand and recall (function), in a logical sequence (flow), and only then shaped (form) | Review every page in that order: findability (nav/search) → reading sequence → visual design; a beautiful page that fails findability is rejected |
| G2 · The spread is the unit | He treated the double-page spread, not the page, as the "visual unit" | Compose each viewport state as a whole (scroll-snap sections), not an endless column of independent cards |
| G3 · Fixed entry corner + traffic signs | Each unit identified in the upper right; numbers, shapes and colours code sections like road signs | Every screen has a fixed identity corner (section code + title); wayfinding code = number + shape + colour, mirrored in navigation and breadcrumbs |
| G4 · Articulated type for scanning | Underlining, size and weight contrast, colour and reversal help the eye search tables and specs | Spec tables and search results: key values reversed or bold, units in grey, labels small caps; test with 5-second find-the-value tasks |
| G5 · Bleed, don't box | He rejected traditional margins and used bleeds for impact | Full-bleed section headers and images; text columns live inside the grid while colour fields and photos run to the edge |
| G6 · Punctuation as structure | The area-code parentheses chunked a long number into readable parts | Input masks and display formats chunk data (phone, card, IBAN, dates) into groups with consistent separators |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** bright primaries and orange with black — approx. `#E8501E`, `#0B5FA5`, `#F6C000`, `#111111`, `#FFFFFF`.
- **Type:** Futura and grotesques, large bold numerals. Free: Jost, Hanken Grotesk, IBM Plex Sans.
- **Form:** triangles, bars, arrows, rectangles enclosing type, photographs.
- **Composition:** dynamic equilibrium of coded blocks; clear zones; strong figure–ground in marks.

## Attitude

Design is the organisation of information for a busy industrial reader — closer to engineering than decoration — yet built on thought and principles, not formulas. He saw the designer's field extending from books to every kind of industrial communication.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `herbert-bayer`, `isotype` — shared conviction that visual systems make information democratic.
- **Productive tension with:** `karel-teige` — his compatriot's poetic collage against his catalogue logic gives information products with personality.
- **Clashes with:** `brutalism` — web brutalism's flat anti-hierarchy undoes the articulated scanning system he built.

## Don't copy

The Sweet's Catalog Service trademark (1942); *Catalog Design Progress* pages; his Družstevní práce covers and the magazine *Žijeme* (1931); his *Build the Town* toy blocks.
