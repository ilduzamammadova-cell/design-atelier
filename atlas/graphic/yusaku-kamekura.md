# Yusaku Kamekura (1915 to 1997)

**Essence:** A few huge symbols, a black ground and engineered light, constructivist geometry made into national emblem.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Symbol stack | The Tokyo 1964 emblem stacks three elements only: a large red disc, the Olympic rings, and gold "TOKYO 1964" in bold condensed letters. | Brand lockup limited to three parts: one dominant geometric shape carrying the brand colour, one small secondary emblem, one line of condensed bold type. Nothing else in the lockup, ever. |
| G2 · Light as subject | For Nikon (1950s) and lighting clients he made light itself the image: prismatic spectra, vertical line fields, glowing concentric rings on black. | Dark-mode hero built from 1px spectral line fields or concentric rings in 2 to 3 close hues on near-black (~#0B0B10); lines animate slowly in brightness, never position. |
| G3 · Stopped motion on black | The second Olympic poster isolated sprinters bursting from the blocks against a black ground (photograph by Osamu Hayasaki), with the emblem in a band beneath. | Product or action photography on pure black, frozen at peak motion, with brand mark and title in a separate solid band below the image, never over it. |
| G4 · Optical vibration | Concentric circles in close-valued greens and blues seem to oscillate and glow. | Loading and "live" indicators as concentric rings in two adjacent hues of equal lightness, pulsing at ≥ 1.5s period; honour `prefers-reduced-motion`. |
| G5 · East-West letter pairing | He paired Japanese characters and Western letters (torn-paper kana beside Bodoni) spelling the same word. | Bilingual headings set as a single visual unit: the two scripts share cap height / baseline and colour, aligned on one axis rather than stacked as translation. |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** sun red ~#D7261E, gold ~#B8975A, black, white; spectral blues, greens and magentas for light themes.
- **Type:** bold condensed grotesques, Bodoni, Japanese Gothic → Oswald or Antonio (condensed), Bodoni Moda, Zen Kaku Gothic New.
- **Form:** discs, rings, line fields, high-contrast photography.
- **Composition:** central, monumental, few elements, strict separation of image and type bands.

## Attitude

Nicknamed "Boss", Kamekura helped found the Japan Advertising Artists Club (1951) and the Nippon Design Center (1960), insisting that graphic design was a profession equal to fine art and essential to industry. He believed symbols could communicate across languages, drawing on both constructivism and the Japanese crest tradition.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `japanese-modernism`, `josef-muller-brockmann`, `otl-aicher`, the Olympic lineage of symbolic, systematic communication.
- **Productive tension with:** `polish-poster-school`, his engineered emblem against hand-cut metaphor: symbolic posters with warmth.
- **Clashes with:** `arts-and-crafts`, handicraft texture undermines his machine-perfect light and geometry.

## Don't copy

The Tokyo 1964 emblem and poster series, Nikon posters, Expo and lighting posters, the *Idea* magazine logo, any of his trademarks.

## Study

- *Trademarks and Symbols of the World* (1965).
- Meggs' History of Graphic Design, chs. 20 and 23; Jeremy Aynsley, *A Century of Graphic Design* (2001).
