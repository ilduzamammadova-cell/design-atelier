# Armin Hofmann (1920 to 2020)

**Essence:** A harmony built from opposites, soft against hard, organic against geometric, black against white, resolved into one taut whole.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Named contrast pair | Each design is driven by one explicit opposition: a blurred, organic dancer photograph against razor-edged capitals (*Giselle*), curve against straight, static against moving. | Give every key screen a declared contrast pair in its spec, e.g. "soft photo / hard type". Execution: a defocused or grainy image beside a heavy grotesque headline with tight tracking; nothing in between. |
| G2 · Black-and-white first | Colour is withheld until the form works in black and white; when it appears it is a single, deliberate accent. | Design every screen in greyscale first. The accent colour token is allowed on ≤ 5% of the surface and only for one element per view (primary action or live state). |
| G3 · Point, line, plane | His teaching reduced visual language to elementary marks and their interactions, replacing pictorial illustration. | Icon and illustration set built only from dots, straight lines and filled planes on a 24px grid; 2px stroke; no perspective, no detail lines. |
| G4 · Letters that interlock | Hand-adjusted, very tight letterspacing and capital ligatures (the Basel theatre logotype) make a word behave like a single object; a word can become a gesture (*Tell* as an arrow aimed at an apple). | Wordmarks and hero headings with optical kerning at −3% to −5% tracking; one letter allowed to act as a pointer or shape relating to the image. |
| G5 · Counterform as positive | The white inside and between shapes is drawn as carefully as the black; open forms let page space flow into a symbol (Expo 64 mark). | Logos and icons left open on one side so the background enters the shape; spacing around marks tuned optically, not by box model. |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** black, white, one occasional red or primary; many works are pure black on white.
- **Type:** Akzidenz-Grotesk capitals, hand-drawn and photographically modified lettering → Archivo (tight), Schibsted Grotesk.
- **Form:** high-contrast photography, geometric letterforms, fields of dots, organic silhouettes cascading in space.
- **Composition:** few elements, extreme scale differences, tension between a dominant shape and a counter-shape.

## Attitude

For Hofmann design was taught and learned through fundamentals; a designer earns expression by first mastering elementary form. Vitality comes from tension, and the designer's job is to bring opposing forces into balance without killing either one.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `emil-ruder`, Basel's typography and form courses were two halves of one curriculum.
- **Productive tension with:** `bradbury-thompson`, process-colour exuberance disciplined by Hofmann's black-and-white rigour gives bold but controlled colour systems.
- **Clashes with:** `memphis`, pattern-on-pattern chroma removes the single contrast that his compositions depend on.

## Don't copy

*Giselle* (1959), *Wilhelm Tell* (1963), Herman Miller poster (1962), Basel Stadttheater logotype (1954), Swiss National Exhibition Expo 64 mark.
