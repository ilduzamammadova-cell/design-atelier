# Pentagram

**Essence:** The idea before the look: a partnership of independent designers who answer each brief with one sharp, often funny, concept.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Idea-led, no house style | There is no Pentagram look; recognisability comes from the intelligence of each solution, derived from the content. | No global decorative motif. Each product area's single distinctive device must be derivable from its own content (a data product's accent shape comes from its own chart). Write the one-sentence idea in the spec before drawing. |
| G2 · The visual pun | Two readings in one form: Fletcher's V&A mark, where an ampersand restores a missing crossbar; a poster where bus passengers become the bodies wearing slippers. | One "double-read" moment per product (an empty state built from the app's own UI parts; a loader formed from the logo's letters). Never more than one per user flow. |
| G3 · Federated partnership | Founded in 1972 by five partners across graphics, product and architecture; partners are peers who lead small teams and the firm grows by adding partners, not layers. | Design system as a federation: a central core (type, grid, spacing, colour) plus one signature component set owned by each product team, capped at one signature per team. |
| G4 · Modern and historical in one toolkit | Clean geometric identities for corporations beside warm historicism for publishers (patterned papers for Faber & Faber). | Theme tokens support a "heritage" skin (serif, patterned paper backgrounds) on the same grid and components as the modern skin; swapping skins must not move a single box. |
| G5 · Exacting craft after the idea | Wit is backed by rigorous photography, illustration, typography and print supervision. | A finish checklist per release: optical alignment of icons, kerning of display type, image crops reviewed at every breakpoint. Ideas ship only at that standard. |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** no fixed palette; typically white space, black, and one decisive colour chosen per client.
- **Type:** whatever the idea needs, Bodoni-style didones (V&A), dot-matrix letters (Reuters), serif-sans pairings (The Guardian masthead by David Hillman) → free: Bodoni Moda, Libre Caslon, Instrument Sans.
- **Form:** simple symbols, letter combinations, found objects photographed straight.
- **Composition:** calm, centred on the idea; little ornament around it.

## Attitude

Pentagram treats design as problem solving with a sense of humour: analyse the brief and the conditions where the work will live, then find the simplest idea that makes people smile and remember. The partnership itself is a design: autonomy for each partner, shared reputation, public programmes of books and lectures.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `paula-scher`, `michael-bierut`, `push-pin-studios`, partners and cousins in idea-driven American wit.
- **Productive tension with:** `studio-dumbar`, idea discipline holding Dutch staged chaos gives energy with a point.
- **Clashes with:** `david-carson`, intuition without a concept and concept without intuition cancel; the result is cleverness nobody can read.

## Don't copy

The V&A logo, the Reuters dot logo, the Star Alliance mark, the Citi logo, the Saks Fifth Avenue pattern, the Public Theater identity, and any partner's specific marks.
