# Erik Spiekermann

**Essence:** Information design with a human voice: legibility engineered for real conditions, typefaces with enough character to be remembered.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Humanist sans for hard conditions | FF Meta began as a typeface for the German Post Office: small sizes, poor printing, open shapes and distinct letters, a warmer answer to Helvetica. | UI and body text in a humanist sans with a generous x-height and disambiguated glyphs (I l 1, 0 O); test at 11px on low-density screens before adopting. |
| G2 · Colour by function | At Düsseldorf Airport (1996) directional and service signs were separated by background colour, with a green specific to that airport. | Wayfinding colour by function: all navigation in one colour family, all information and service messages in another; the two are never swapped. |
| G3 · One map before many products | MetaDesign reconciled East and West Berlin's separate transit systems into one diagram and information system after reunification. | When merging products, design the single shared map first (taxonomy, navigation, naming), then the visuals; one diagram style for all products. |
| G4 · Corporate voice through type | For Deutsche Bahn (DB Type, with Christian Schwartz) and others, a proprietary family carries the brand across every touchpoint. | The brand is expressed mainly by its type family (text sans, serif, condensed) used everywhere from tickets to app; the logo is secondary. |
| G5 · Type for tight spaces | Later families such as Axel targeted spreadsheets and tables, keeping similar characters distinct. | Dense tables use tabular figures, a narrow width and distinct 0/O and 1/l; 28px rows; numbers right-aligned to the decimal. |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** transit yellows and reds, airport green, neutral warm greys; information colours used as codes.
- **Type:** FF Meta, ITC Officina, FF Info, FF Unit, DB Type, Nokia Sans, Fira Sans (Firefox OS, with Ralph du Carrois) → free: Fira Sans (his own, open source), Source Sans 3, Atkinson Hyperlegible.
- **Form:** pictograms on modular tiles, transit diagrams, tidy rules.
- **Composition:** dense but disciplined information layouts.

## Attitude

Trained in art history and self-taught in type, Spiekermann argues that typography is the visible form of language and should serve people in real situations. He distanced himself from over-rational German design and found Helvetica too bland. He founded MetaDesign (1979) and FontShop (1989), later Edenspiekermann, and runs an experimental letterpress workshop in Berlin.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `swiss-international`, `otl-aicher` — German and Swiss information traditions.
- **Productive tension with:** `designers-republic` — rigorous information design dressed in techno irony gives data-rich interfaces with personality.
- **Clashes with:** `david-carson` — expressive illegibility in wayfinding simply fails people.

## Don't copy

The FF Meta, ITC Officina and DB Type outlines; the BVG Berlin network map; the Düsseldorf Airport pictograms and signs; Nokia Sans.
