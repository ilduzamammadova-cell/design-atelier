# Olle Eksell (1918–2007)

**Essence:** Modernism with a human face — a pair of eyes, a light brush line and a plain argument that good design is good economy.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Two eyes as a whole identity | In 1956 he won the competition to redraw the Mazetti cocoa company's old eye motif; the streamlined pair of eyes then ran across packaging, vans, billboards and ads. | Build the brand on a minimal facial cue — two dots or two simple eyes — that appears in the loader, avatars, empty states, packaging and notifications; it can blink or look toward new content. |
| G2 · Light brush line | His posters and *Graphis* cover (1951) use a quick, springy brush or pen line, influenced by the School of Paris, with flat colour and white space. | Spot illustrations drawn with one confident line weight (brush-like, slightly varying), flat fills in two colours, at least 60% white space; no shading. |
| G3 · Design = economy | His book *Design = Ekonomi* (1964) argued to Swedish business, in plain terms, that design is part of economic life, not decoration. | Each design decision in specs carries a one-line business rationale (cost saved, conversion, support tickets avoided); design reviews include a numbers slide. |
| G4 · Warm domestic modernism | Working in a culture of "beautiful everyday goods", he gave packaging and identities a friendly, home-like tone. | Warm palette: off-white ~#F4EFE6, cocoa ~#5A3A29, soft orange ~#E58A3A, muted blue-grey; rounded but not bubbly shapes; friendly, plain copy. |
| G5 · Abstract or figurative as needed | He moved between abstract geometry and playful figuration depending on the client. | Allow two illustration registers in one system — geometric (for data/finance areas) and figurative line (for help/community) — sharing palette and line weight. |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** black, white, cocoa browns, warm orange, red accents.
- **Type:** classical serifs and simple grotesques; hand-drawn titles → Fraunces (soft), Karla, Libre Caslon.
- **Form:** eyes, faces, brush figures, circles and half-circles.
- **Composition:** open, centred or loosely asymmetric, lots of air, single focal motif.

## Attitude

Eksell studied in Stockholm and at the Art Center in Los Angeles and returned determined to end Swedish graphic design's isolation. He believed design belonged at the centre of economic and cultural life, and that modernism should be humane, light and approachable.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `scandinavian-modern`, `paul-rand` — humane, witty modernism for everyday products.
- **Productive tension with:** `swiss-international` — his warm line and eyes placed in a strict grid make friendly but orderly consumer products.
- **Clashes with:** `brutalism` — raw, cold surfaces cancel his gentleness.

## Don't copy

The Mazetti eyes (1956), the SAFFT poster (1954), the *Graphis* no. 37 cover (1951), the *Design = Ekonomi* cover (1964).
