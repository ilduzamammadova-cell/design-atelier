# Stefan Sagmeister

**Essence:** Design meant to move people: letters made from bodies, objects and time, carrying personal statements.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Letters from real matter | Type built from bananas, coins, branches, sugar, even lettering cut into his own skin for an AIGA Detroit lecture poster (1999). | Campaign headline as a photograph of letters physically built from a material tied to the content; the same words exist as live HTML text for search and screen readers. |
| G2 · Handwriting on the subject | For Lou Reed (1996) song lyrics were handwritten across the singer's face. | In personal or editorial contexts, annotate photographs with real scanned handwriting (SVG), 12 words at most, never over eyes, always with sufficient contrast. |
| G3 · Maxims as content | His series *Things I have learned in my life so far* turned diary sentences into typographic works and installations. | Brand voice expressed as short first-person maxims placed in empty states, loading moments and transitions; each maxim is true for the company and dated. |
| G4 · Outline fixed, fill from context | The Casa da Música identity in Porto took the building's faceted shape as a logo filled with colours sampled from each event. | A logo whose outline never changes but whose fill is sampled from the current content (cover image, event palette) at render time. |
| G5 · Time as material | Works that decay (a wall of bananas changing colour) and a studio that closes for a year-long sabbatical every seven years. | One element that changes over time by design: an illustration that ages with the account or the season, with the change history visible to users. |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** whatever the material gives, skin, fruit yellow, coin copper, plus bright exhibition colours.
- **Type:** handwriting, material-built letters, inflatable and projected words beside a plain sans → free: Caveat or Reenie Beanie for handwriting accents, Familjen Grotesk for text.
- **Form:** photographs of objects and bodies, installations, packaging with hidden layers.
- **Composition:** idea-driven, often a single striking photograph with type inside the scene.

## Attitude

Sagmeister wants design that touches the heart as well as informing. He mixes personal content with commissions, treats his own life as a field of experiments (happiness, beauty, time off) and argues that beauty is functional, not decoration. He worked with Jessica Walsh as Sagmeister & Walsh (2011 to 2019).

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `m-m-paris`, `studio-dumbar`, hand-made images and staged physical scenes.
- **Productive tension with:** `experimental-jetset`, a dry Helvetica system holding one handmade, emotional element gives structured warmth.
- **Clashes with:** `erik-spiekermann`, handmade lettering in wayfinding or data fails legibility where it matters most.

## Don't copy

The AIGA Detroit poster, the Lou Reed *Set the Twilight Reeling* poster, the Casa da Música logo, the *Things I have learned…* installations, the banana wall, his album packaging for the Rolling Stones and David Byrne.
