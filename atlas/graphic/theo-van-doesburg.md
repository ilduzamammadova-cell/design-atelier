# Theo van Doesburg (1883 to 1931)

**Essence:** The movement's engine, a rigid system-builder who also wrote Dada poetry under a pseudonym, proving order and anarchy can be one practice.

He founded and largely financed *De Stijl* (1917 to 31), taught an unofficial De Stijl course in Weimar that drew Bauhaus students (1921 to 22), convened the Congress of Constructivists and Dadaists (Weimar, 1922), toured Holland with Schwitters, and published Dada poetry as I. K. Bonset.

## Genes

## Visual facts

- **Color:** primaries plus black, white and grey; red as the favoured second ink.
- **Type:** his own square-module letters; grotesques in tight blocks; mixed sizes and weights for Bonset's Dada poems. Free: Archivo Black, Space Grotesk.
- **Form:** rectangles, bars, diagonal counter-planes.
- **Composition:** type held at the four corners of an implied rectangle (1922 cover); 45° counter-compositions.

## Attitude

Dada would demolish the old order and De Stijl would build on the cleared site; art should dissolve into architecture and everyday life. Gropius found him too dogmatic for a Bauhaus post, yet his outsider teaching shifted the school toward geometry.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `de-stijl`, `el-lissitzky`, Lissitzky designed an issue of *De Stijl* (1922).
- **Productive tension with:** `kurt-schwitters`, his real collaborator: a structured primary grid that admits Merz collage gives systematic but witty event identities.
- **Clashes with:** `memphis`, random pattern-on-pattern muddies the structural logic the diagonal depends on.

## Don't copy

His *De Stijl* covers and 1919 alphabet drawings; the *Kleine Dada Soirée* poster (1922, with Schwitters); *Counter-Composition* paintings; the Café Aubette interiors (Strasbourg, 1926 to 28); the cover of *Grundbegriffe der neuen gestaltenden Kunst* (1925, with Moholy-Nagy).
