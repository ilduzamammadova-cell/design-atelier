# Paul Schuitema (1897 to 1973)

**Essence:** Advertising as engineering, overprinted photographs, arrows and type assembled on the press bed to argue a product's precision.

Trained as a painter, he turned to design in the early 1920s. His key client was the Rotterdam firm of P. van Berkel (meat, and patent scales and slicers), plus the printer C. Chevalier and the Dutch PTT. He joined the Ring circle and taught for about three decades at the Royal Academy of Art in The Hague, shaping postwar Dutch design.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · The arrowed headline | A Berkel brochure uses one huge word (ZOO, "so") with arrows to two claims, producing a double headline | Feature callout pattern: one giant keyword on the product image with 2 to 3 leader lines/arrows to short claims; replaces bullet lists on spec pages |
| G2 · Overprint image and colour | Solid colour planes and type printed over photographs; the overlap carries information | Product photos with a solid band in `multiply` carrying the key numbers; two inks only (black + red) across the whole campaign |
| G3 · Design on the production bed | Layouts were assembled directly from type and rules at the press from his sketch, not finished drawings | Design high-fidelity directly in code with real components and data; the static mock is a sketch, the build is the design |
| G4 · Photography as evidence | Machines shown frontal, sharp, close; objectivity as persuasion | Orthographic product views and macro detail crops on neutral ground, each crop paired with one measurable claim |
| G5 · Rigid axes, one diagonal | Strict horizontals and verticals with a single diagonal movement per layout | Grid-locked page with exactly one diagonal element (band, arrow or rotated headline) per viewport |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** black and red on white or cream, `#111111`, `#E2231A`, `#F6F1E7`.
- **Type:** grotesque capitals, very large single words, arrows and rules. Free: Archivo Black, Familjen Grotesk, Work Sans.
- **Form:** arrows, bars, halftone product photos, overprinted planes.
- **Composition:** rigorous horizontal/vertical structure, one diagonal thrust, layered printing.

## Attitude

Design is an objective, socially responsible tool, not personal art; persuasion should rest on visible facts about the product. Teaching was part of the work, he passed the constructivist method to generations of Dutch designers.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `piet-zwart`, `new-typography`, the Dutch wing of functional typography.
- **Productive tension with:** `plakatstil`, a single iconic object hero annotated by Schuitema-style arrows and numbers gives product pages that are both emblematic and factual.
- **Clashes with:** `psychedelic`, swirling colour drowns the precision his arrows point to.

## Don't copy

The Berkel "ZOO" brochure and other Berkel brochures and posters; the Berkel trademark; the PTT stamp poster (1932); his Chevalier printing advertisements.
