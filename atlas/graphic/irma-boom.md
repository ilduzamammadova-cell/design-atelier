# Irma Boom

**Essence:** The book as a sculptural object: thickness, edges, paper and even the absence of ink carry the story.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · The edge carries meaning | Her books use edges, thickness and the closed block as surfaces that reveal images and structure before a page is opened. | List items carry a 4 to 6px coloured side edge encoding category or period, so a stacked list reads as a pattern from a distance, like the fore-edge of a book. |
| G2 · Time runs backwards | The *SHV Think Book 1996 to 1896* (2,136 pages, five years in the making) tells a company's century starting from the present. | Timelines default to newest-first with random-access date anchors instead of numbered pages; readers can enter anywhere and move in either direction. |
| G3 · Ink-less relief | The Chanel N°5 book (2013) is entirely white, its 300 pages embossed without ink, 5cm thick in a nod to the name. | A ceremonial "blind emboss" theme: white on white, form shown only by 1px highlight and shadow. Reserved for one moment (a certificate, a launch page) with an accessible high-contrast toggle. |
| G4 · Dimensions from content | Format, thickness and paper are decided by what the book is about, not by standard sizes. | Component proportions derived from a content fact (a card's aspect from the data's own ratio, a page's length from the archive's span); the rationale is written next to the token. |
| G5 · Scale extremes | She makes monumental volumes and tiny books, using scale itself as expression. | One deliberate scale extreme per product: a full-bleed monumental view paired with a very small, precise colophon (9 to 10px, still contrast-compliant). |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** white and natural paper tones, coloured edges and bright accents; colour often printed on the block rather than the page.
- **Type:** varied per book, often small, classical and modest against strong material decisions → free: EB Garamond, Newsreader, Public Sans.
- **Form:** embossing, die-cuts, deckled and printed edges, thick blocks, unusual bindings.
- **Composition:** sequence and rhythm across hundreds of pages more than single spreads.

## Attitude

Boom came to books by accident while studying painting and worked at the Dutch government printing office before founding her studio in 1991. She treats the book's physicality as its future, works for years in close dialogue with clients, and keeps artistic autonomy within commissions. She received the Gutenberg Prize, and MoMA holds dozens of her books.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `karel-martens`, `kenya-hara`, Dutch tactile book culture and white material emptiness.
- **Productive tension with:** `muriel-cooper`, physical depth versus screen depth; book-like edges in a zoomable interface.
- **Clashes with:** `punk-graphics`, instant photocopied urgency and years-long craft undermine each other.

## Don't copy

The *SHV Think Book*, the Chanel N°5 book, *Weaving as Metaphor* (Sheila Hicks, 2006), the Rijksmuseum identity (2013).
