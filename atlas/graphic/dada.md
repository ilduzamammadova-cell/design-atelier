# Dada

**Essence:** Meaning by collision — chance, found scraps and clashing type set against each other to puncture authority.

Born at the Cabaret Voltaire in Zurich (1916) with Hugo Ball, Tristan Tzara, Jean Arp and Richard Huelsenbeck; spread to Berlin (Hausmann, Höch, Heartfield, Grosz), Hanover (Schwitters' offshoot Merz), Paris and New York (Duchamp, Man Ray). It dissolved by about 1922–23, but its methods became permanent tools.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Chance as method | Arp's chance-dropped papers, Tzara's cut-up poems: a procedure, not taste, places things | Seeded generative layout: a per-session seed randomises rotation (±8°) and offset (±24px) of decorative elements only; content order, tap targets and reading flow never move |
| G2 · Found, not drawn | Tickets, ads, newspaper type and bureaucratic forms reused as raw material | Brand visuals built from the product's own "found" artefacts — receipts, real screenshots, error logs, user photos — collaged into editorial headers |
| G3 · Type as performance | Every word in a different size, weight or direction; sound poems (Ball's *Karawane*, 1916) read as scores | One expressive headline per campaign where each word takes a face from a fixed set of six; `aria-label` carries the plain sentence for screen readers |
| G4 · Parody the form | Dada mimicked manifestos, official notices and advertising to mock them | A deliberately absurd "official form" or terms page that makes a point (e.g. about dark patterns) — only where the joke never blocks the task |
| G5 · Readymade recontext | Duchamp moved an everyday object into a new frame and it became new | Promote a mundane system component — a receipt, progress bar, 404 — to hero brand element |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** black on newsprint or cream with red — `#121212`, `#E9E2D0`, `#C8102E`.
- **Type:** whatever was in the case: grotesques, wood type, slabs, Fraktur fragments, often upside down. Free: Rubik Mono One, Alfa Slab One, UnifrakturMaguntia, IBM Plex Mono, Libre Bodoni.
- **Form:** torn edges, cut-outs, rubber stamps, pointing-hand dingbats, rotated lines.
- **Composition:** scattered and multidirectional; illustrations dropped onto the page without hierarchy.

## Attitude

After the slaughter of 1914–18, rational bourgeois culture had forfeited its authority; nonsense, chance and mockery were honest answers. Destruction cleared ground for builders — Van Doesburg saw Dada and De Stijl as two halves of one programme.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `punk-graphics`, `postmodern-new-wave` — DIY collage and typographic rule-breaking.
- **Productive tension with:** `de-stijl` — a strict orthogonal grid hosting one seeded-random collage layer gives order with a pulse.
- **Clashes with:** `corporate-identity` — consistency manuals neutralise the shock that is Dada's only power.

## Don't copy

Ball's *Karawane* layout; Hausmann's *ABCD* and *The Art Critic*; Höch's *Cut with the Kitchen Knife*; Duchamp's *Fountain* and *L.H.O.O.Q.*; the *Le Coeur à barbe* cover (1922); covers of *DADA* and *Der Dada*.
