# Jonathan Barnbrook

**Essence:** Type design as political commentary: inscriptional letters and corporate gloss repurposed to say uncomfortable things.

## Genes

| Gene | The idea | In UI / product terms |
|---|---|---|
| G1 · Names that argue | His typefaces carry loaded names and readings (Exocet, Prozac, False Idol, Patriot, Mason, first called Manson); the name is part of the design. | Name tokens and components by intent, not appearance (`ink.authority`, `type.whisper`, `surface.evidence`), and publish the names; the system's vocabulary states its values. |
| G2 · Inscription meets digital | Rooted in English lettering and Eric Gill's legacy, he made machine-cut stone inscriptions with ironic texts and translated chiselled capitals into fonts. | Titling caps with sharp wedge serifs and +0.1em tracking for monumental headings only; never lowercase in the titling face; body in a neutral sans. |
| G3 · Erase to reuse | For David Bowie's *The Next Day* (2013) a plain white square was placed over an earlier famous cover: meaning by cancellation. | A "redaction" component: a solid block over part of an image or text with a label explaining what was removed or changed, used in archives, changelogs and corrections. |
| G4 · Corporate grammar, critical content | Spoof identities and slogans in pristine corporate layouts, including collaborations with Adbusters. | Use the clean grammar of finance dashboards (tables, tickers, KPI tiles) for civic or non-commercial data; the irony comes from content, never from added ornament. |
| G5 · Few shapes, many letters | Prozac was built from a minimum of shapes, rotated to make other characters. | Icon set generated from 4 to 6 base shapes by rotation and reflection only; the generation rules are documented so new icons can be derived, not drawn. |

## Visual facts

- **Color:** black, white, gold, blood red; the *Blackstar* (2016) black-and-white star glyph vocabulary.
- **Type:** Exocet, Mason, Priori, Bastard, Infidel, Nylon and others from his foundry Virus (1997) → free: Cinzel (inscriptional caps), Cormorant SC, IBM Plex Mono.
- **Form:** stars, crosses, stencil marks, chemical formulae, grids over photographs.
- **Composition:** monumental, centred, heavy contrast between black fields and small exact type.

## Attitude

Barnbrook chose to stay a small studio rather than serve large corporations, and has argued that designers are responsible for what their skills promote. His work mixes idealism with cynicism and dark humour. He came through Why Not Associates and the Young British Artists scene (Damien Hirst's 1997 book) before long collaborations with David Bowie.

## Blends

- **Amplifies:** `adbusters`, `metahaven`, critique of power through its own visual codes.
- **Productive tension with:** `pentagram`, a clear corporate idea carrying a subversive message works like a Trojan horse.
- **Clashes with:** `memphis`, pastel playfulness neutralises the critique.

## Don't copy

The Bowie covers *Heathen* (2002), *The Next Day* (2013) and *Blackstar* (2016); the outlines of Exocet, Mason and Prozac; the Damien Hirst book *I Want to Spend the Rest of My Life Everywhere…* (1997); the Mori Art Museum identity.
